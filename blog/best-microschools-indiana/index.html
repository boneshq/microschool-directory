River Montessori High School — South Bend

River Montessori High School is a Montessori microschool in South Bend serving grades 9-12. Opened in 2020, the school bills itself as a mixed-income Montessori high school and uses sliding-scale tuition tied to family income. Students earn an Indiana Core 40-aligned diploma, so credits transfer toward state graduation requirements.

Community-based learning and a hybrid 9th-grade design help students who are new to Montessori. River Montessori High School sits at 333 West Colfax Avenue in downtown South Bend.

Published tuition runs $18,250 to $22,250, but the sliding scale means many families pay less. Ask the school how the income-based rate applies to your situation.

Kainos Microschool — Fort Wayne

Kainos Microschool is a personalized microschool in Fort Wayne serving students ages 7 to 18. Founded by Jill Haskins, the program builds an individual learning plan for each student and aims at children who struggle in a conventional classroom. A Christian foundation anchors the program, which still accepts students from different backgrounds.

Personalized paths replace the rigid grade structure through middle and high school. Its campus, at 4516 Parnell Avenue, sits in Fort Wayne, the Indiana city with the most listings in our directory.

This program does not post tuition publicly, so contact the school for current rates and any state-funding options.

Virtue Prep Academy — Anderson

Virtue Prep Academy is a project-based microschool in Anderson serving grades 6-8. The program pairs project-based learning with character and leadership development, and the school was enrolling for the 2025-2026 year at the time of review. Virtue Prep Academy runs its campus at 2277 N Madison Avenue in Anderson.

That middle-grades focus makes Virtue Prep Academy a fit for families looking at the years right before high school. Project work replaces a lecture-first format, and the stated mission centers on integrity and real-world leadership.

The school does not list tuition online. Reach Virtue Prep Academy at 765-757-2590 to confirm pricing and enrollment.

How much do microschools cost in Indiana?

Listed Indiana microschool tuition runs from about $3,200 to $18,250 per year, based on schools in our directory that publish a price. Many smaller microschools do not post a number, and some, like River Montessori High School, use sliding-scale or income-based pricing. Expect to ask each school directly for a current figure.

Indiana families have two main state-funding routes. One is the Indiana Education Scholarship Account, which funds students with disabilities and their siblings; the other is the Indiana Choice Scholarship voucher, which covers tuition at participating non-public schools. A microschool has to be an approved provider or a voucher-accepting school for either program to apply. See the state's official page on the Indiana Choice Scholarship Program for eligibility and current amounts.

How to choose a microschool in Indiana

Start with grade range and location, then match the learning model to your child. Tour the school, ask about class size and the daily schedule, and find out how the school tracks progress. Confirm tuition and any state-funding approvals before you enroll. A short list of two or three schools you have visited beats a long list you have only read about.

Use these steps when you compare Indiana microschools:

  • Filter the microschools in Indiana directory by your city and your child's grade.
  • Read up on the model. If a term like Montessori or project-based is new, check the glossary of terms.
  • Visit in person and ask how a typical day runs, who teaches, and how many students share a room.
  • Ask whether the school accepts the Indiana ESA or Choice Scholarship, and what your out-of-pocket cost would be.

Frequently asked questions

How many microschools are in Indiana?

microschool.directory lists 16 microschools in Indiana across 13 cities. Fort Wayne has the most with 3, followed by Indianapolis with 2. The list spans Montessori, project-based, learner-driven, and forest/nature models.

How much do microschools cost in Indiana?

Listed Indiana tuition runs from about $3,200 to $18,250 per year, based on schools in our directory that publish a price. River Montessori High School in South Bend uses sliding-scale tuition tied to family income. Many smaller microschools do not post a number, so ask each school directly.

Can Indiana families use state funding for a microschool?

Some can. The Indiana Education Scholarship Account funds students with disabilities and their siblings, and the Indiana Choice Scholarship covers tuition at participating non-public schools. A microschool must be an approved provider or voucher-accepting school for the funding to apply, so confirm eligibility with the school and the state.

How do I choose a microschool in Indiana?

Start with location and grade range, then match the learning model to your child. Visit in person, ask about class size, daily schedule, and how the school measures progress. Confirm tuition and whether the school accepts state funding before you enroll.

Are Indiana microschools legitimate schools?

Microschools are small private schools or homeschool programs, not public schools, so they are not state-accredited by default. Each one sets its own model and standards. Verify the school's registration, curriculum, and any state-funding approvals, and tour the program before enrolling.
