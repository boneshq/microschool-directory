Microschooling & school choice in Montana

The directory currently covers two microschools across two Montana cities: Hamilton and Bozeman. Both follow a learner-driven approach, the Acton Academy affiliate model, so the listings here share one teaching philosophy rather than a wide spread of types. Published tuition is not posted for either school, which is common for small independent programs that quote rates per family. Montana has expanded school-choice options in recent years, including education savings account legislation aimed at qualifying students. Parents should confirm current eligibility and award amounts with the Montana Office of Public Instruction or the program administrator before counting on that money. Either Bozeman or Hamilton is a reasonable starting point depending on which part of the state you live in.

Finding the right fit in Montana

Start with the city closest to you: Bozeman in the south-central part of the state, or Hamilton in the Bitterroot Valley. Both listed schools run on the Acton Academy model, so a tour will look similar at each, with students working independently and in small groups across mixed ages. Reach out to the school directly for tuition, since neither posts a public rate. Ask whether they accept any Montana education savings account or scholarship funds, and what paperwork the family handles versus the school. On a visit, watch how the guides support students who are stuck, how the day is structured, and how the school measures progress without traditional grades.

Montana microschool FAQ

How many microschools are in Montana?

The directory lists two microschools in Montana, one in Hamilton and one in Bozeman. Each is an Acton Academy affiliate running a learner-driven program. The directory grows as more schools are added, so check back for new listings in other Montana towns.

How much does a microschool cost in Montana?

Neither Montana school publishes a set tuition figure. Small independent programs like these usually quote a rate per family based on age and schedule, so you will need to contact Acton Academy Hamilton or Bozeman Innovation Academy directly. Ask whether sibling or part-time rates apply.

Can I use an ESA or scholarship for a Montana microschool?

Possibly. Montana has moved to expand school choice, including education savings account legislation for qualifying students. Eligibility rules and award amounts change, so confirm your status with the Montana Office of Public Instruction or the specific program before you assume a school will accept those funds.

What types of microschools are in Montana?

The two listed schools use the learner-driven Acton Academy model. That means mixed-age groups, students setting their own goals, and Socratic discussion rather than lecture-and-test instruction.

Are both Montana microschools the same kind of program?

Both Montana microschools are Acton Academy affiliates, which keeps the format consistent across the two cities. If you want a particular method, such as Montessori or a faith-based co-op, you may need to look at nearby states or wait for new Montana listings, since the current options share one approach.
