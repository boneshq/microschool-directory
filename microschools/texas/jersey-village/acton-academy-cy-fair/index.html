About this school

Acton Academy Cy-Fair operates a microschool in Houston, Texas, serving students from kindergarten through twelfth grade. The institution utilizes the Learner-Driven Classroom model, where students design their own educational paths with guidance from adult guides. This approach emphasizes self-paced learning, real-world problem solving, and peer collaboration to foster independence and critical thinking skills across all grade levels within the campus.
