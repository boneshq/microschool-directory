About this school

Nature's Schoolhouse Microschool in Cedar Park, Texas, serves students from PreK through fifth grade in an intentionally small private setting. The curriculum integrates academic instruction with outdoor experiences to foster creativity and critical thinking. This approach emphasizes a low-stress environment where learners spend significant time exploring the natural world, distinguishing the school through its focus on connecting education with nature rather than traditional classroom structures.
