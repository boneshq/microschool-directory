About this school

Forest STEAM Academy serves grades 6 through 12 in Spring Branch, Texas. Located near a river within a wooded setting, the school focuses on kinesthetic learning for middle and high school students. The curriculum emphasizes experience-based education where academic progress is assessed via portfolios and meaningful projects rather than traditional testing. This approach aims to develop critical thinking and problem-solving skills through direct engagement with the natural environment.

Meet the founder: During

The founder of Forest STEAM Academy previously co-owned and managed an IT business for nearly two decades, specializing in project management. Her background includes extensive community mentorship through leading Girl Scout troops, coaching sports, directing cheer squads, and teaching art and outdoor lessons.
