About this school

Sage Academy Granbury is a microschool located in Granbury, Texas, serving students from kindergarten through fourth grade. The institution focuses on providing small-group instruction tailored to early childhood development. Its distinct approach emphasizes personalized learning pathways and close student-teacher interaction within a compact educational setting designed for foundational academic growth.
