About this school

Lake Travis Christian Academy is an Acton Academy located in Travis County, Texas, serving students from PreK through 12th grade. This microschool utilizes the learner-driven Acton model, emphasizing personalized education plans and self-directed learning within a Christian framework. The institution offers a distinct alternative to traditional schooling by focusing on individual student agency and tailored academic growth across all grade levels.
