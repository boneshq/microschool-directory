About this school

Athena Micro-Academy of Austin serves students in grades six through twelve within Austin, Texas. The school operates year-round with term breaks, offering personalized academic support and a daily Spanish immersion block named Voces. Its distinct Athena’s Council program combines direct instruction with project-based learning to develop leadership and organizational skills. Students may propose interest clubs, attend Intercession Camps, and access after-school programs or appointment-based tutoring.

Meet the founder: Mily

Mily Pérez founded Athena Micro-Academy of Austin. A Texas-born educator with a M.Ed. in Educational Leadership, she is a certified teacher and principal who previously served as Head of School at her alma mater and taught at St. Andrew's Episcopal School and The Kinkaid School.
