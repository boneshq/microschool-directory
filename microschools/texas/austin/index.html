Microschooling & school choice in Austin

Austin hosts 14 microschools and homeschool co-ops in this directory, placing it among the largest Texas hubs alongside Houston, San Antonio, and Cedar Park. Tuition ranges from $1,050 to $15,500 annually across these listings. Learning models include Learner-Driven, Microschool, Project-Based, and Montessori approaches. Texas passed Senate Bill 2 in 2025 to create Education Freedom Accounts launching in 2026-27. These accounts provide approximately $10,000 per student at accredited private schools, up to $11,500 for students with disabilities, and up to $2,000 annually for homeschoolers.
