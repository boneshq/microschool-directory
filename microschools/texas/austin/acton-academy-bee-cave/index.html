About this school

Acton Academy Bee Cave operates as a microschool in Bee Cave, Texas, serving students from PreK through 12th grade. The institution distinguishes itself through a learner-driven model where students set their own goals and pace their education. This approach emphasizes individualized learning paths and self-directed inquiry, allowing each student to progress according to their unique interests and developmental needs rather than following a standardized curriculum.
