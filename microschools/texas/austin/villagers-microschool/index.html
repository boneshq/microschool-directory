About this school

Villagers Microschool serves first through fifth graders in Austin, Texas. The curriculum emphasizes core academics alongside practical life skills, including time management, public speaking, and technology literacy. Students engage in traditional subjects like reading and math while developing critical thinking and communication abilities. The program supports personal projects and business ideas through individual mentoring. Flexible enrollment options allow families to integrate real-world learning experiences into the standard school day for a comprehensive educational approach.

Meet the founder: Meredith Orf

Meredith Orf founded Villagers Microschool. A Southwestern University summa cum laude graduate with a BA in Music and English, she previously tutored, taught through Teach For America, and ran a piano studio before launching this educational venture.
