About this school

The Legacy & Leadership Collective operates as a fully online private microschool serving grades PreK through 12th in Texas. Students follow personalized learning paths tailored to individual strengths and pace, with parents actively selecting curriculum resources within a centralized digital hub. The program accommodates varied instructional preferences, ranging from traditional book-based methods to technology-integrated approaches. Additionally, the school facilitates annual virtual World Tours for global perspective building and hosts yearly nationwide gatherings to foster community connections among its dispersed student body.
