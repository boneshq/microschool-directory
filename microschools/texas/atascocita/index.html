Empowerment Leadership Academy

📍 Atascocita, TX
Project-Based

Empowerment Leadership Academy is a microschool in Atascocita, Texas, serving grades six through twelve. It specializes in supporting gifted but inconsistent learners, including those with executive functioning challenges like ADHD. The program integrates rigorous academics with intentional leadership training and character development. Students receive personalized support to build accountability, communication skills, and self-discipline within a structured environment designed to foster critical thinking and personal growth alongside academic achievement.
