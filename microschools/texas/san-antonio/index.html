Learner-Driven

Acton Academy North San Antonio serves students from PreK through eighth grade in San Antonio, Texas. This microschool distinguishes itself by utilizing the Learner-Driven Model, which empowers children to take charge of their own educational journey. Aristotle’s Garden Micro-Academy

📍 San Antonio, TX
Project-Based

Aristotle’s Garden Micro-Academy in San Antonio, Texas, serves fourth through sixth graders through a mastery-based curriculum grounded in Socratic philosophy.
