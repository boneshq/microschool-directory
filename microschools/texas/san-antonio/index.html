Acton Academy North San Antonio

📍 San Antonio, TX
Learner-Driven

Acton Academy North San Antonio serves students from PreK through eighth grade in San Antonio, Texas. This microschool distinguishes itself by utilizing the Learner-Driven Model, which empowers children to take charge of their own educational journey. The institution focuses on individualized pacing and self-directed learning, allowing students to navigate their curriculum at a speed that suits their unique developmental needs rather than following a traditional standardized schedule.

Aristotle’s Garden Micro-Academy

📍 San Antonio, TX
Project-Based

Aristotle’s Garden Micro-Academy in San Antonio, Texas, serves fourth through sixth graders through a mastery-based curriculum grounded in Socratic philosophy. The program emphasizes deep reading, writing, and discussion within small groups of up to twelve students. This structure prioritizes intellectual depth over rapid pacing, offering a specialized environment for learners who may require additional support or distinct pedagogical approaches compared to traditional schooling models.

Calmecac In Lak'Ech

📍 San Antonio, TX
Project-Based

Located in San Antonio, Texas, Calmecac In Lak'Ech serves students in grades five through twelve. The school distinguishes itself by integrating Indigenous knowledge with S.T.E.A.M education to support social-emotional learning and holistic wellbeing. Its curriculum emphasizes land-based learning and intergenerational instruction, aiming to foster cultural identity preservation while developing critical thinking and community well-being among its student body.

Inheritance Hall

Texas passed Senate Bill 2 in 2025, creating Education Freedom Accounts that begin in the 2026-27 school year and provide state funds families can put toward private-school tuition or approved education costs. San Antonio microschool FAQ

How many microschools are in San Antonio?

This directory lists seven microschools and homeschool co-ops in San Antonio. The mix leans toward project-based programs, with one learner-driven school and one general microschool also included.

How much does a microschool cost in San Antonio?

Cost depends entirely on the individual school, since tuition is not standardized across programs. Some microschools and co-ops charge a few thousand dollars a year while others run higher, so ask each school you contact for its current rate and any sibling or part-time options.

What learning models do San Antonio microschools use?

Most of the seven are project-based, where students learn through extended hands-on work rather than a fixed lecture schedule. Acton Academy North San Antonio uses a learner-driven model that puts students in charge of much of their own pace, and Primer Microschool - San Antonio is listed as a general microschool.

Can San Antonio families use a Texas ESA or scholarship for a microschool?

Yes, with conditions. Texas created Education Freedom Accounts through Senate Bill 2 in 2025, and funding begins in the 2026-27 school year. Check the state's eligibility rules and confirm with each San Antonio school whether it accepts ESA funds.

How do I enroll my child in a San Antonio microschool?

To enroll, contact the schools directly to ask about open seats, since small programs fill limited spots and may keep waitlists. Visit in person when you can, meet the staff, and ask how they group ages and track progress before you commit.
