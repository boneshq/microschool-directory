About this school

Inheritance Hall is a K–8 microschool located in San Antonio, Texas. Founded on biblical principles, the institution integrates faith into every subject to provide personalized academic excellence. The curriculum emphasizes spiritual growth, creativity, and collaborative learning across grade levels. Students explore real-life challenges within a nurturing environment designed to help them discover their purpose while developing confidence in their identity through a Christ-centered educational framework.

Meet the founder

Destiny founded Inheritance Hall. A San Antonio native, she is a mother, minister, and mentor with experience in homeschooling, after-school programs, daycares, and youth ministry.
