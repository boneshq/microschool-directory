Fort Worth microschool FAQ

How many microschools are in Fort Worth?

Fort Worth has three microschools and homeschool co-ops listed in this directory. Two use a Project-Based model and one is Learner-Driven. That makes it one of several Texas cities with options alongside larger hubs.

How much does a microschool cost in Fort Worth?

Typical tuition for Fort Worth listings sits around $10,450 a year. Each program sets its own price, and some charge more or less depending on grade level, days per week, and what is bundled in. Ask each school what tuition covers before comparing figures.

What models or types of microschools does Fort Worth have?

The three Fort Worth schools split between two models. Two run Project-Based learning, where students work through real projects rather than fixed lessons, and one is Learner-Driven, giving children more say over their own pace. Acton Academy Fort Worth is the Learner-Driven example on the list.

Can Fort Worth families use a Texas ESA or scholarship for a microschool?

Texas passed Senate Bill 2 in 2025, which creates education savings accounts beginning in the 2026-27 school year. Eligible families can apply the funds toward private school tuition or approved homeschool expenses. Acceptance is not automatic at every school, so confirm with each program and check current eligibility with the Texas Education Agency.

How do I enroll my child in a Fort Worth microschool?

To enroll, contact each Fort Worth school directly, since most run small classes and fill seats early. Tour the campus, ask about start dates and waitlists, and request the enrollment paperwork. If a school is full, ask whether it keeps a waitlist or can suggest a nearby program such as Innovations Leadership Academy.
