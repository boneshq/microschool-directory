About this school

Acton Academy Fort Worth serves students in grades PreK through 6th Grade in Fort Worth, Texas. This microschool utilizes the Learning Model for self-directed education, allowing learners to progress at their own pace within a collaborative environment. The curriculum emphasizes mastery of core subjects alongside social-emotional learning and real-world problem-solving skills tailored to individual student interests and developmental stages.
