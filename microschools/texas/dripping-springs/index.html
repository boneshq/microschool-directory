Microschooling & school choice in Dripping Springs

Texas passed Senate Bill 2 in 2025, creating Education Freedom Accounts that launch in 2026-27. These accounts provide approximately $10,000 per student at accredited private schools and up to $2,000 for homeschoolers. Families may be able to use the funds for eligible expenses and should confirm with the school.
