About this school

Brilliant Minds Christian Academy is a private institution located in Humble, Texas, serving students from Pre-K through second grade. The school integrates Christian faith with its academic curriculum, emphasizing spiritual growth alongside character development and critical thinking. It utilizes individualized instruction to support early childhood education, aiming to build strong academic foundations while fostering moral integrity within a supportive community environment for young learners.

Meet the founder: With

Sharita Bourgeois-Clark, founder of Brilliant Minds Christian Academy, has over 16 years of education experience. Previously nominated for Teacher of the Year, she homeschooled her two children for four years each before founding her microschool.
