About this school

Endless Possibilities Academy is a private microschool in Humble, Texas, serving grades PreK through 5th Grade. The institution emphasizes academic growth, creativity, and character development within a personalized environment. Distinctive features include small class sizes and a flexible instructional approach that utilizes hands-on learning methods to foster meaningful relationships among students and build a strong sense of community.

Meet the founder: Erica Peterson

Erica Peterson, a mother and daycare industry educator, founded Endless Possibilities Academy. She draws on professional experience and personal inspiration to provide a personalized, supportive micro-school environment for children who thrive in smaller settings with flexible learning opportunities.
