About this school

PivotED is a microschool in Conroe, Texas, serving students from kindergarten through twelfth grade. The institution utilizes a mixed-age environment where progression is based on mastery rather than age or speed. Curriculum blends core academics with practical life skills, including financial literacy and problem-solving. Small group sizes allow for personalized support, enabling learners to advance at their own pace while developing independence and critical thinking abilities within a structured yet flexible framework.

Meet the founder: Tommi

Tommi, founder of PivotED, holds a Bachelor’s in Psychology from Sam Houston State University and a Master’s in Education from Texas A&M University. With over 13 years of experience teaching early childhood through elementary grades, she established the school to address student needs in traditional systems.
