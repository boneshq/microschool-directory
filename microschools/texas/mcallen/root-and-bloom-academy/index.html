About this school

Root and Bloom Academy serves PreK through 3rd-grade students in McAllen, Texas, offering a Montessori-inspired microschool model. The program emphasizes personalized, holistic education within small class sizes to accommodate individual learning needs. Instructional approaches utilize gentle parenting principles rather than traditional reward systems to foster student empathy and resilience. This distinct methodology aims to cultivate creativity and independent problem-solving skills in a supportive academic environment.

Meet the founder: Sunaina Chugani

Sunaina Chugani Marquez spent 18 years researching consumer psychology at Baruch College, San Diego State University, and UTRGV before co-founding Root and Bloom Academy with her husband, Matt Marquez.
