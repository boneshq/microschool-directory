About this school

Located in Katy, Texas, Mirus Academy serves students in grades 8 through 12. Established in 2014, this microschool maintains an enrollment of approximately 25 to 30 students supported by five faculty members. The institution functions as a hybrid model between traditional schooling and homeschooling, focusing on college-preparatory academics within a small-group educational setting.
