About this school

Grace Academy and Tutoring serves students in grades six through twelve in Bay City, Texas. This faith-based, accredited private micro school offers a Christ-centered educational environment alongside a strong STEM-oriented curriculum. The institution utilizes small learning groups and individualized instruction tailored to each student’s specific strengths and goals. This approach aims to support academic development while fostering character, integrity, and critical thinking skills within a personalized setting.

Meet the founder: My

Christie Meyerdirk founded Grace Academy and Tutoring, a STEM school designed to foster curiosity and critical thinking. Her entrepreneurial background drives her mission to create an engaging learning environment where students explore science and technology while building confidence and problem-solving skills.
