Academy of Business & Innovation

📍 McKinney, TX
Microschool

Academy of Business & Innovation is a fully virtual private high school in McKinney, Texas, serving grades 9 through 12. It offers a mastery-based curriculum aligned with CLEP standards, allowing students to earn up to 60 college credits. The program blends weekly live instruction with collaborative labs and discussion-based seminars led by business mentors. Graduates receive a professional portfolio alongside their transcript, focusing on real-world decision-making skills and peer cohort collaboration.

📍 McKinney, TX
Microschool

Acton Academy McKinney serves students from PreK through twelfth grade in McKinney, Texas. This microschool utilizes the Acton Academy model, which emphasizes self-directed learning and learner-driven curricula within a mixed-age environment. The institution focuses on developing critical thinking and problem-solving skills through project-based work and personalized mentorship, distinguishing itself by offering an alternative to traditional classroom structures for its diverse student body across all grade levels.

Morgan Preparatory Academy for Boys

📍 McKinney, TX
Microschool

Morgan Preparatory Academy for Boys in McKinney, Texas, serves students in grades one through four. This therapeutic microschool tailors its curriculum to the developmental needs of young boys, emphasizing structured learning environments that integrate movement and hands-on activities. The program focuses on building emotional regulation, accountability, and character development alongside academic excellence, providing a specialized educational approach for this specific age group.

Microschooling & school choice in McKinney

Three microschools are listed in McKinney, and all three follow the microschool model: Acton Academy McKinney, Academy of Business and Innovation, and Morgan Preparatory Academy for Boys. Tuition is set by each program individually, so ask the school directly for current rates and any sibling or early-enrollment discounts. On the funding side, Texas passed Senate Bill 2 in 2025, creating Education Freedom Accounts that begin in the 2026-27 school year. The program is expected to provide roughly $10,000 per student toward an accredited private school and a smaller amount for homeschool families. Because the accounts are new, confirm with the Texas Comptroller and with each school whether they plan to accept the funds.

McKinney microschool FAQ

How many microschools are in McKinney?

McKinney has three microschools listed in this directory: Acton Academy McKinney, the Academy of Business and Innovation, and Morgan Preparatory Academy for Boys. All three run as standalone programs rather than campuses of a larger chain. Each sets its own enrollment size, so seats can be limited.

How much does a microschool cost in McKinney?

Tuition is set by each McKinney program separately, and several do not post prices online. You will get an accurate number by contacting the school and asking about the full year, plus any registration fees or discounts. Cost can vary widely depending on grade level and hours.

What types of microschools are in McKinney?

Every school currently listed in McKinney uses the microschool model, which means small enrollment and a flexible, often mixed-age structure. Acton Academy McKinney leans on self-directed learning, while Morgan Preparatory Academy for Boys focuses on a single-gender setting. Visiting each one shows how differently the same label can play out.

Can McKinney families use a Texas ESA or scholarship for a microschool?

Texas created Education Freedom Accounts through Senate Bill 2 in 2025, with the program opening for the 2026-27 school year. Once active, families may be able to apply the funds toward tuition at an accredited private school. Whether a given McKinney microschool participates is up to that school, so ask before you count on it.

How do I enroll my child in a McKinney microschool?

New families usually start by booking a tour and asking about open seats for the coming year, since these programs are small and fill quickly. If McKinney options are full, look at nearby Collin County towns such as Frisco, Allen, and Plano for additional microschools. Joining a waitlist early keeps your choices open.
