About this school

Acton Academy McKinney serves students from PreK through twelfth grade in McKinney, Texas. This microschool utilizes the Acton Academy model, which emphasizes self-directed learning and learner-driven curricula within a mixed-age environment. The institution focuses on developing critical thinking and problem-solving skills through project-based work and personalized mentorship, distinguishing itself by offering an alternative to traditional classroom structures for its diverse student body across all grade levels.
