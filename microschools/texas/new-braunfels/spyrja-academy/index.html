About this school

Spyrja Academy serves students in grades six through twelve within New Braunfels, Texas. The institution emphasizes interdisciplinary learning by connecting content areas to address real-world problems and community service projects. This approach integrates academic instruction with a focus on belonging and purpose-driven education. Additionally, the school plans to expand its operations to Dallas, with a new location scheduled to open in 2027.

Meet the founder: Jourdan Simmang

Jourdan Simmang is the director of Spyrja Academy, a New Braunfels microschool. He brings fifteen years of Texas teaching experience, having served as a teacher, curriculum writer, department head, and assistant principal.
