About this school

Brighter Futures Microschool in Arlington, Texas, serves students in grades 1 through 6 with learning, language, or social differences requiring individualized instruction. The school utilizes small, skill-based groups of three to five students. Trained teachers deliver explicit, systematic, and evidence-based education across all subjects, allowing each child to work at their appropriate academic level while developing confidence and real-world skills within a focused environment.

Meet the founder: Brittany Lopez

Brittany Lopez holds degrees in Early Childhood, Elementary, and Special Education. A certified special education teacher and Professional Educational Therapist, she founded Brighter Futures Microschool after over a decade of experience supporting diverse learning needs across various instructional settings.
