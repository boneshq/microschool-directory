Microschooling & school choice in Louisiana

The directory currently covers three schools across three Louisiana cities: Lake Charles, Youngsville, and Chalmette. Two follow a project-based approach, where students work through hands-on projects, and one runs as a learner-driven Acton Academy. Each program publishes its own tuition, so families confirm rates directly with the school. On the funding side, Louisiana launched LA GATOR, an education savings account that routes state money to families for tuition and approved education expenses at participating nonpublic schools. Eligibility and award amounts depend on the year and household income, so check the current rules through the Louisiana Department of Education before you enroll.

Finding the right fit in Louisiana

Start with the city closest to you: Lake Charles in the southwest, Youngsville near Lafayette, or Chalmette just outside New Orleans. Reach out to each school to ask about openings, daily schedule, and how a project-based or learner-driven classroom actually runs day to day. If cost is a concern, look into LA GATOR, the state education savings account that can cover tuition and approved expenses at participating nonpublic schools; the Louisiana Department of Education lists current eligibility and how to apply. On a visit, ask about group sizes, how teachers track progress without traditional grades, and which subjects students cover each week. Request references from current families and confirm whether the school accepts LA GATOR funds before you commit.

Louisiana microschool FAQ

How many microschools are in Louisiana?

Three microschools and homeschool co-ops are listed in Louisiana right now, located in Lake Charles, Youngsville, and Chalmette. The directory grows as new programs open and register, so it is worth checking back. Most current listings use a project-based model.

How much does a microschool cost in Louisiana?

Costs are set by each individual school rather than a statewide rate. Two of the three Louisiana listings run a project-based model, and one is a learner-driven Acton Academy. Contact the school directly for its current tuition and any sibling or early-enrollment discounts.

Can I use an education savings account in Louisiana?

Yes. Louisiana runs LA GATOR, an education savings account program that gives eligible families state funds for tuition and approved education costs at participating nonpublic schools. Award amounts and eligibility depend on household income and the school year, so confirm the current rules with the Louisiana Department of Education and ask whether your chosen school accepts the funds.

What types of microschools operate in Louisiana?

The Louisiana listings include two project-based schools, such as Motivating Minds in Youngsville, and one learner-driven program, JOY: An Acton Academy in Lake Charles. Project-based schools build learning around hands-on projects, while learner-driven schools give students more say in pacing and topics.

Which microschool is closest to New Orleans?

New Orleans sits next to Chalmette, where FIVE Micro School operates, so families in the metro area have a nearby option in St. Bernard Parish. Lake Charles and the Lafayette area cover the southwest part of the state. Because the three schools are spread out, your nearest option depends on where you live.
