Microschooling & school choice in Aurora

This directory currently lists five microschools and homeschool co-ops in Aurora. The model mix leans Montessori, with four of the five schools using that method and one running a learner-driven format. Colorado does not operate a statewide private-school ESA or voucher, so most families here pay tuition directly or rely on individual school scholarships and payment plans. Finding the right fit in Aurora

Start by comparing the five Aurora listings side by side: note which use Montessori versus the learner-driven model, and match that to how your child learns best. Tuition begins near $1,440 per year, but ask each school for a full fee schedule, since costs shift with age group, hours, and any sibling discounts. Colorado has no statewide ESA or voucher, so plan to pay tuition directly; check your district's education agency for any current homeschool enrichment funds or local scholarship options. When you visit a school, ask about student-to-teacher ratio, daily schedule, how they handle mixed ages, and what state reporting they file. Request to observe a class in session rather than relying on a tour alone. Confirm enrollment deadlines early, since smaller programs fill their limited seats quickly.

Aurora microschool FAQ

How many microschools are in Aurora?

This directory lists five microschools and homeschool co-ops in Aurora, Colorado. Four use a Montessori approach and one follows a learner-driven model. New programs open regularly, so the count can change between school years.

How much does a microschool cost in Aurora?

A typical Aurora microschool starts around $1,440 per year based on the listed figure, though prices vary widely by program. Full-time schedules, older grade levels, and add-on services usually raise the total, so ask each school for its current fee schedule. Many programs offer payment plans.

What models or types of microschools are available in Aurora?

The Aurora listings are mostly Montessori, with four of the five schools using that method. The remaining school runs a learner-driven model, where students help set their own goals and pace. Both formats keep class sizes small.

Can Aurora families use a state ESA or scholarship for a microschool?

Colorado does not run a statewide ESA or voucher program, so families generally pay microschool tuition out of pocket. Some individual schools offer their own scholarships or sliding-scale tuition, and your district may have homeschool enrichment options worth checking with the state education agency.

How do I enroll my child in an Aurora microschool?

The Sunflower School and Flame Lily Montessori are two of the Aurora Montessori options listed here. Contact each school directly to ask about open seats and enrollment windows, since smaller programs often have waitlists and fill early.
