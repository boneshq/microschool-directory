Microschooling & school choice in Colorado

Colorado reports 10,367 full-time homeschooled students in 2025-26, up 19.5% since 2022 and 5.5% from the prior year. This directory lists 12 microschools and homeschool co-ops in the state, with Aurora as a primary hub. Listed tuition ranges from $1440 to $16060 per year. Models include Montessori, Learner-Driven, and Microschool approaches. Colorado has no state-level private school choice program such as an ESA or voucher. Open enrollment, magnet, and charter schools remain the available school-choice options for families.
