Nebraska microschool FAQ

How many microschools are in Nebraska?

Three microschools and homeschool co-ops appear in our Nebraska directory right now. They sit in two cities, with two in Omaha and one in Lincoln. The count changes as new programs register, so check back if you don't see one near you.

How much does a microschool cost in Nebraska?

None of the three publish a set tuition figure, so the best move is to call each school and ask about current rates and any aid they offer. Costs depend on grade level, hours, and the model a school runs.

Can I use a scholarship or ESA for a Nebraska microschool?

Funding through a scholarship or education-savings account may be possible, but Nebraska's rules have changed in recent years. Contact the Nebraska Department of Education to confirm what is available this year and whether your family qualifies.

What types of microschools does Nebraska have?

The Nebraska listings split into two learning models. Learner-driven schools like Acton Academy Omaha and Acton Academy Lincoln let students set goals and work at their own pace, while the project-based option centers lessons on hands-on projects. Both keep class sizes small.

Which Nebraska cities have microschools?

Omaha is the easiest place to start, since it holds two of the three listings, including Identity Preparatory Academy. Lincoln has the remaining option, so families in the capital have a nearby choice without driving to Omaha.
