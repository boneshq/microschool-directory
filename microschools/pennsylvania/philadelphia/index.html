Microschooling & school choice in Philadelphia

Philadelphia hosts five microschools and homeschool co-ops. These listings feature Montessori, Learner-Driven, and Microschool models. Tuition varies by program; families should ask each school for current rates. Pennsylvania funds private-school tuition through the EITC and OSTC tax-credit scholarship programs. These programs awarded over 100,000 scholarships in 2023-24 averaging about $2,700 to $3,200. The state has no ESA covering homeschool expenses. Families may be able to use these tax credits for eligible expenses and should confirm with the school.
