About this school

Mosaic Yonder is a microschool in Clifton Park, New York, serving grades 9 through 12. The institution offers a non-traditional high school environment centered on project-based learning and interdisciplinary study. Students engage in cooperative collaboration to explore interests through dynamic projects that connect various subjects. This approach prioritizes critical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving over a rigid curriculum, allowing learners to take ownership of their education through hands-on experiences and mentorship within the community.

Meet the founder: Barbara Bemis

Barbara Bemis founded Mosaic Yonder in Albany after 30 years as a math teacher. Her credentials include decades of classroom experience, which she now applies to creating a student-driven microschool focused on hands-on, real-world learning.
