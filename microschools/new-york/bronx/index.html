Microschooling & school choice in Bronx

This directory lists three microschools in the Bronx, and all three use the Montessori model. They include Gloria Alston Children's Learning Center, The Bronx Chrysalis Montessori School, and The Rose School. None of these programs publish a fixed tuition figure, so you will need to contact each one for current rates. New York does not run a statewide ESA or voucher program for private school tuition, which sets it apart from states like Florida or Arizona. Families in the Bronx typically pay Montessori tuition out of pocket or apply for privately funded scholarships offered by individual schools. Check the New York State Education Department for any current options that may apply to your situation.

Finding the right fit in Bronx

Start by reviewing the three Bronx Montessori programs in this directory side by side: Gloria Alston Children's Learning Center, The Bronx Chrysalis Montessori School, and The Rose School. Because none post tuition online, call or email each one to ask about yearly cost, payment plans, and any sibling or financial-aid discounts. Since New York has no statewide ESA, ask whether a school offers its own scholarship or sliding-scale tuition. When you visit, watch a classroom in session and ask how mixed-age groups work, what the daily schedule looks like, and how teachers track each child's progress. Confirm the student-to-teacher ratio and the ages each program accepts before you commit.

Bronx microschool FAQ

How many microschools are in the Bronx?

This directory lists three microschools in the Bronx, and all three are Montessori programs. They are Gloria Alston Children's Learning Center, The Bronx Chrysalis Montessori School, and The Rose School. Each runs as a small, independent school rather than a large campus.

How much does a Bronx microschool cost?

Tuition for Bronx Montessori microschools is not posted publicly, so you will need to ask each school for its current rate. Costs usually depend on the child's age, the length of the program day, and any aid the school offers. Request a full fee breakdown, including registration and materials, when you reach out.

What models or types do these schools use?

Every microschool listed here for the Bronx follows the Montessori method, with mixed-age classrooms and hands-on, self-directed work. You will not find other model types in this particular city listing. The three schools differ in size and age range, so compare them directly.

Can I use a state ESA or scholarship in New York?

New York does not operate a statewide ESA, voucher, or tax-credit scholarship for private school tuition, so there is no state account to apply Bronx Montessori tuition against. Some individual schools run their own scholarship or sliding-scale aid. Check the New York State Education Department for any current programs before you assume what is available.

How do I enroll my child at a Bronx microschool?

To enroll, contact the school directly, since spots in these small programs fill quickly. Ask The Rose School or The Bronx Chrysalis Montessori School about waitlists, start dates, and what documents they need. If none of the three fit, look at neighboring areas of New York City for more Montessori and microschool options.
