Microschooling & school choice in Oregon

Our Oregon directory currently shows one microschool, based in Newberg, just southwest of Portland. The single listing follows a learner-driven model, an approach where children direct much of their own work with adult guides instead of standing at the front of a classroom. Listed tuition is about $600, which is low compared with traditional private schools across the state. Oregon does not run a statewide education savings account or voucher program, so most families pay out of pocket or piece together private scholarships. Parents should check with the Oregon Department of Education for current homeschool registration rules and any local scholarship options.

Can I use an ESA or scholarship in Oregon?

Oregon does not offer a statewide education savings account or voucher program as of 2026. Most families pay tuition themselves or look for private scholarships. Check with the Oregon Department of Education for the latest on homeschool rules and any funding options.

What types of microschools are in Oregon?

The listed Oregon program uses a learner-driven model. In that setup, students pursue individual goals and work with adult guides rather than sitting through whole-class lessons. Forge: An Acton Academy in Newberg is the example currently in our directory.

What is Forge: An Acton Academy like?

Forge: An Acton Academy is part of the Acton network, which runs learner-driven, mixed-age studios. Students often work on real projects and set weekly goals with a guide. Visiting in person is the best way to see whether that style fits your child.
