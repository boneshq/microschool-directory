About this school

Heart Learning Collective is a microschool located in White Bear Lake, Minnesota, serving students from Kindergarten through fifth grade. The institution distinguishes itself through individualized instruction within small class sizes, emphasizing project-based learning to develop critical thinking. Students engage in hands-on activities designed to encourage exploration and self-expression, fostering an environment that prioritizes creative skill development alongside academic growth for young learners.

Meet the founder: Kathleen Amstutz

Kathleen Amstutz is a co-founder of Heart Learning Collective with 16 years of public school teaching experience. She collaborates with co-founder Tamara Morett, who brings 27 years of district education background and eight years of K-5 special education co-teaching expertise.
