About this school

Nurturing Minds is a Microschool that is dedicated to providing a hands on and personalized experience for its learners. Our goal is to provide an educational alternative for families who are looking for a small, warm group of learners. Our goal is to provide an educational alternative for families that do not align with ‘traditional’ learning environments by providing a safe, warm environment for a diverse group of learners. Traditional learning settings are not always a good fit for many children, and we wanted to be able to provide a safe space for children to be themselves and learn in a way that best fits them. We are not just focused on academics, but on the whole child. Including social skills, emotional skills, cleaning, life skills, and so much more.
