About this school

Acton Academy Red Rock operates in the Las Vegas Valley, Nevada, serving students from kindergarten through twelfth grade. This microschool utilizes the Acton Learning model, characterized by learner-driven education where students progress at their own pace. The institution distinguishes itself by integrating guided mentorship with individualized learning paths, allowing pupils to master academic subjects while developing essential life skills within a collaborative environment.
