Microschooling & school choice in Miramar

The directory currently lists three schools in Miramar. Two are Microschool programs and one follows the Montessori method, so the mix here leans toward small-enrollment, independent learning settings. Exact tuition varies by school and is best confirmed directly with each program, since published figures change year to year. Florida runs two main school-choice programs that apply here: the Family Empowerment Scholarship for private school tuition and the Personalized Education Program (PEP) for families building a more customized, part-time or homeschool-style plan. Both are administered through state-approved funding organizations, and microschools often qualify as eligible providers. Parents should verify each school's current participation before assuming scholarship money can be used.

Finding the right fit in Miramar

Start by comparing the three Miramar listings side by side: Casa Ranch Montessori, Protea Montessori, and Horizon Learning each take a different approach, so look at age ranges, group size, and weekly schedule. Two of the three run as Microschools, which usually means small mixed-age cohorts and a shorter or more flexible week than a standard private school. If cost is a factor, look into Florida's Family Empowerment Scholarship and the Personalized Education Program, then ask each school whether it accepts those funds and how reimbursement works. On a visit, ask how many students share each teacher, how the school handles reading and math levels that differ across ages, and what a typical day looks like. Also ask about enrollment timing, since smaller programs fill seats quickly and may keep a waitlist. Request references from current families so you can hear how the school fits day to day.

Miramar microschool FAQ

How many microschools are in Miramar?

This directory lists three microschools and co-ops in Miramar. Two operate as Microschool programs and one is a Montessori school. Listings change as new programs open or register, so it is worth checking back for recent additions.

What does a microschool in Miramar cost?

Tuition is not standardized across the three Miramar schools, and several factors affect it: full-time versus part-time enrollment, age of the child, and any included materials or meals. Florida scholarship funds can offset much of the cost for eligible families, so ask each school for its current rate sheet and whether it accepts state funding.

What types of microschools are available here?

The Miramar listings cover two formats: Microschool programs and a Montessori school. Microschools group a small number of students across mixed ages with one or two teachers, while Montessori uses self-directed work with specialized materials. Casa Ranch Montessori and Protea Montessori represent the Montessori option, and Horizon Learning is one of the Microschool programs.

Can I use a Florida ESA or scholarship for a Miramar microschool?

Yes. Florida offers the Family Empowerment Scholarship for private school tuition and the Personalized Education Program for more customized or homeschool-style plans. Funds are administered through state-approved organizations, and many microschools qualify as participating providers. Confirm with the specific school that it accepts the scholarship you plan to use.

How do I enroll, and what if the Miramar schools are full?

Contact each school directly to ask about open seats and start dates, since smaller programs in Miramar often enroll on a rolling basis and keep waitlists. If the three listed schools are full or not the right fit, widen your search to nearby Broward cities such as Pembroke Pines or Hollywood, where additional microschools and Montessori programs operate.
