Microschooling & school choice in Delray Beach

Three microschools in Delray Beach appear in this directory. Two use a Forest or Nature model, including Cocoplum Nature School and Nature Education Cooperative, and one, Space of Mind, runs a project-based program. Florida funds private and microschool tuition through the Family Empowerment Scholarship and the Personalized Education Program, an education savings account for homeschooled students. Both are administered by approved scholarship organizations such as Step Up For Students, and award amounts are set each year by the state. Parents can apply the funds toward tuition, curriculum, and some learning materials.
