About this school

Located in Lithia, Florida, RISE Learning Studio serves students from kindergarten through twelfth grade. The institution operates as a hybrid microschool offering specialized programs for lower primary, upper primary, middle grades, and high school support, alongside part-time homeschool enrichment services. It distinguishes itself by prioritizing individual learning plans to address diverse educational needs, while also providing homeschool assessments and portfolio reviews.

Meet the founder: Melissa Palmer

Melissa Palmer founded RISE Learning Studio. A Tampa native, she holds a USF bachelor’s degree and a Saint Leo University Master’s in Education. Her background includes roles as a classroom teacher, interventionist, special education coordinator, and instructional coach.
