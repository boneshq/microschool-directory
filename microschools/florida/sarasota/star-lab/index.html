About this school

Star Lab is a Montessori-inspired microschool in Sarasota, Florida, serving students from Pre-K through 4th grade. Located in historic Newtown, the institution emphasizes individualized learning plans, daily arts integration, and frequent movement breaks to support holistic development. The school offers healthy meals, weekly parent conferences, and monthly academic showcases to track student progress. It accepts Step Up scholarships for eligible families seeking a personalized elementary education environment.

Meet the founder: Alison Rini

Alison Rini, a teacher and school leader with over 20 years of experience, holds three Columbia University degrees in Neuroscience, Curriculum, and School Leadership. She founded Star Lab, a Newtown microschool serving K-3 students, to apply research-based practices from diverse educational models.
