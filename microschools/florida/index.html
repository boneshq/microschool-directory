Microschooling & school choice in Florida

Florida’s home education count reached 155,532 students in 2024, reflecting a 46 percent increase since 2020. The directory lists 62 microschools and homeschool co-ops across hubs like Tampa, Fort Lauderdale, and Miami. Tuition for these schools ranges from $3,200 to $13,500 annually. Models include learner-driven, project-based, and Montessori approaches. Florida’s Personalized Education Program provides eligible K-12 residents with an education savings account averaging about $8,000. Families may be able to use it for eligible expenses and should confirm with the school.

Finding the right fit in Florida

Florida families have 62 microschools and homeschool co-ops to review. Tuition ranges from $3,200 to $13,500 annually. Learning models include Microschool, Learner-Driven, Project-Based, and Montessori. Eligible K-12 residents may use a Personalized Education Program account averaging $8,000 for tuition or materials. Families should confirm eligibility with the school. Visit hubs in Tampa, Fort Lauderdale, Jacksonville, Miami, or Clermont to observe classroom dynamics and assess fit before enrolling.

Florida microschool FAQ

How many microschools are in Florida?

The directory lists 62 microschools and homeschool co-ops in Florida. Major hubs include Tampa, Fort Lauderdale, Jacksonville, Miami, and Clermont. Tuition among listed schools ranges from $3,200 to $13,500 per year. Learning models include microschool, learner-driven, project-based, and Montessori approaches.

Can you use a Florida ESA or scholarship for a microschool?

Florida’s Personalized Education Program provides eligible K-12 students an education savings account averaging about $8,000. Families may be able to use it for eligible expenses and should confirm with the school. The directory lists 62 microschools and homeschool co-ops in Florida.

How much does a microschool cost in Florida?

Tuition for listed microschools and homeschool co-ops in Florida ranges from $3,200 to $13,500 per year. These 62 schools operate across major hubs including Tampa, Fort Lauderdale, Jacksonville, Miami, and Clermont.

What types of microschools are in Florida?

Florida lists 62 microschools and homeschool co-ops. These listings include Microschool, Learner-Driven, Project-Based, and Montessori models. Tuition ranges from $3,200 to $13,500 annually. Major hubs include Tampa, Fort Lauderdale, Jacksonville, Miami, and Clermont.

Is homeschooling growing in Florida?

Florida had 155,532 students in home education in 2024, a 46% increase since 2020. However, the reported count dipped about 1.7% in 2024-25 because students taking education savings accounts are no longer counted as homeschoolers.
