About this school

Faith Builders Christian Academy is a microschool located in Boynton Beach, Florida, serving students from kindergarten through second grade. The institution offers a faith-centered curriculum emphasizing literacy, STEM education, and character development. Small class sizes facilitate personalized instruction and hands-on learning experiences designed to support academic growth alongside social and spiritual development for young learners.

Meet the founder: Cherline Collot

Cherline Collot holds a Master’s degree in Educational Leadership and has over ten years of teaching experience. She founded Faith Builders Christian Academy as its director to provide faith-centered, individualized support for students’ academic, social, emotional, and spiritual development.
