Microschooling & school choice in Palm Beach

Three schools serve Palm Beach in our current listings. Two are independent microschools and the third, Bridges Christian Learning Center, follows a faith-based approach. Class sizes at this scale tend to stay small, which means a single visit usually lets you meet the teacher who would work with your child. Florida families can apply scholarship funds through the Family Empowerment Scholarship and the Personalized Education Program (PEP), both administered by approved funding organizations such as Step Up For Students. Eligibility and award amounts change yearly, so confirm current figures before you enroll. Tuition varies by program, and several microschools set rates that scholarship money can offset in part or in full.

Finding the right fit in Palm Beach

Start by opening the three Palm Beach listings side by side and noting which models fit your child: a secular microschool like Grandview Perspective or Connecting with the Plant World, or a faith-based program. Reach out to each one directly to ask about openings, since seats at small schools fill quickly. To use Florida scholarship money, apply through Step Up For Students for the Family Empowerment Scholarship or the Personalized Education Program, then confirm the school accepts those funds. On a visit, ask about the daily schedule, the student-to-teacher ratio, how they handle different grade levels in one room, and how they report progress to parents. Bring your questions about cost in writing so you can compare answers later.
