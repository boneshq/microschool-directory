Microschooling & school choice in Fort Lauderdale

This directory lists 8 microschools and homeschool co-ops in Fort Lauderdale. Seven of them are structured as microschools, and one, Acton Academy Fort Lauderdale, uses a learner-driven model where students set their own goals and pace. Tuition is set by each school individually, so confirm current rates directly with the programs you contact. Florida families can apply for the Personalized Education Program, a state-funded education savings account for homeschooled students, or the Family Empowerment Scholarship for those enrolling in private microschools. Both are administered through approved scholarship organizations and can offset tuition, curriculum, and other approved costs. Award amounts and eligibility change year to year, so check the current rules before you budget.

Finding the right fit in Fort Lauderdale

Start by shortlisting a few of the 8 Fort Lauderdale schools that match your child's age and the model you want, such as a structured microschool or the learner-driven approach at Acton Academy. Reach out to each one for current tuition, since rates are not standardized and vary by program. If you plan to use Florida's Personalized Education Program or Family Empowerment Scholarship, apply through an approved scholarship organization early and ask whether the school accepts those funds. On a visit, ask about group size, the daily schedule, how teachers track progress, and what happens when a child falls behind or moves ahead. Request to see a typical day in person rather than relying on a tour script. Compare your notes across schools before committing to enrollment.

Fort Lauderdale microschool FAQ

How many microschools are in Fort Lauderdale?

This directory lists 8 microschools and homeschool co-ops in Fort Lauderdale. Seven are structured as microschools and one, Acton Academy Fort Lauderdale, runs a learner-driven program. New options open regularly, so the live listings are the best place to check current numbers.

How much does a microschool cost in Fort Lauderdale?

Tuition varies by school and is not standardized across Fort Lauderdale, so there is no single citywide figure. Each program sets its own rates based on hours, grade levels, and what is included. Contact the schools on your shortlist directly for current pricing and any sibling or scholarship discounts.

What kinds of microschools and models are available in Fort Lauderdale?

The Fort Lauderdale listings are mostly independent microschools, which are small programs that often blend grade levels and keep class sizes low. One school, Acton Academy Fort Lauderdale, uses a learner-driven model where students direct much of their own work. Others, such as Colossal Academy and Primer Microschool, follow their own microschool formats.

Can Fort Lauderdale families use a Florida ESA or scholarship for a microschool?

Yes. Florida offers the Personalized Education Program for homeschooled students and the Family Empowerment Scholarship for those attending private microschools. These are state-funded education savings accounts run through approved scholarship organizations, and they can cover tuition and other approved expenses. Confirm with each school whether it accepts the funds, since participation is not automatic.

How do I enroll my child in a Fort Lauderdale microschool?

Enrolling is handled school by school in Fort Lauderdale, so there is no central application for the directory listings. Pick the programs that fit your child, then ask each about open seats, start dates, and waitlists. Schools like Colossal Academy and Permission to Succeed Microschool can walk you through their own enrollment steps and required paperwork.
