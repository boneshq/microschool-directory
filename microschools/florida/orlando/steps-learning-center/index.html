About this school

Steps Learning Center in Orlando serves students from kindergarten through eighth grade who have moderate to significant support needs. The microschool offers a neuro-affirming program that combines adapted academics, social-emotional learning, independent living skills, and on-site therapies within small group settings. This ability-centered approach focuses on individual strengths and communication styles rather than rigid traditional models.

Meet the founder: Alexandra Batista

Alexandra Batista founded Steps Learning Center to support ESE students. She holds double bachelor’s degrees in elementary and exceptional student education from the Interamerican University of Puerto Rico and possesses National Virtual Teacher Association certification, alongside seven years of experience in elementary schools and tutoring programs.
