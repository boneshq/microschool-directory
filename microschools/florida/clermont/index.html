Microschooling & school choice in Clermont

Four microschools and homeschool co-ops operate in Clermont. Two are listed as microschools, one follows a learner-driven approach, and one is project-based, so the local mix skews toward small, independent programs rather than a single dominant brand. Florida funds school choice through the Family Empowerment Scholarship and the Personalized Education Program, an education savings account for students whose families choose to educate them outside a traditional school. Eligible K-12 students can use those accounts toward tuition, curriculum, and approved services. Award amounts vary by year and student, so families should confirm current figures with Step Up For Students, the nonprofit that administers Florida's scholarships.

Finding the right fit in Clermont

Start by comparing the four Clermont listings side by side: River Clermont Academy, Lionheart Labs, Inspiring Curious Minds, and the Acton-affiliated learner-driven program. Note which use a microschool, learner-driven, or project-based structure, since that shapes daily routine and group size. For funding, check whether your child qualifies for Florida's Personalized Education Program or Family Empowerment Scholarship through Step Up For Students, then ask each school whether it accepts those accounts and how payment works. On a visit, ask about student-to-teacher ratio, how progress gets measured, what a typical day looks like, and how the school handles different grade levels in one room. Request a trial day or sample week if the school offers one, and talk with current parents about how the program has worked for their kids.
