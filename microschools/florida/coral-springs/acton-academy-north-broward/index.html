About this school

Acton Academy North Broward operates in Coral Springs, Florida, serving students from kindergarten through twelfth grade. This microschool distinguishes itself by utilizing a learner-driven educational model where students set their own goals and progress at individualized paces. The curriculum emphasizes self-directed learning, collaborative projects, and the development of practical life skills within a community-focused environment tailored to diverse academic needs.
