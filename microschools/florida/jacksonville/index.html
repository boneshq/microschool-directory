Imago Dei Education Institute

📍 Jacksonville, FL
Project-Based

Imago Dei Education Institute serves students in grades 6–12 in Jacksonville, Florida. This homeschool-aligned program supports neurodivergent learners and those rebuilding confidence through mastery-based instruction and personalized pacing. The school provides academic coaching and executive-function support within a small, relationship-centered environment. Families maintain legal homeschooling status while partnering with the institute for consistent instruction and accountability in a Christ-centered community.

Visionary Leadership Academy

📍 Jacksonville, FL
Project-Based

Visionary Leadership Academy is a microschool located in Jacksonville, Florida, serving students in grades 5 through 12. The institution specializes in healing-centered, trauma-responsive education, catering to learners from diverse backgrounds including traditional and home-school settings. It distinguishes itself by offering flexible enrollment pathways such as the Thrive Track, Grow Track, and LEAP PASS options. This approach prioritizes emotional wellness alongside academics within an intentionally small learning environment designed for personalized attention.
