Microschooling & school choice in Boca Raton

Boca Raton currently lists 6 schools in this directory. Five of them run as microschools, the kind of small program that keeps class sizes low and mixes ages across a single room or two. The remaining option is a homeschool co-op, where families share teaching and meet on set days. Florida funds a large share of private and home education through the Family Empowerment Scholarship, and the Personalized Education Program (PEP) lets parents of non-enrolled students draw on an education savings account for tuition, curriculum, and approved costs. Award amounts and eligibility shift year to year, so confirm current figures with Step Up For Students before you budget.

Finding the right fit in Boca Raton

Start by comparing the 6 Boca Raton listings on the things that matter to your family: grade range, daily schedule, group size, and teaching approach. Schools like Nova Leadership Academy and The Grow Co-op sit close together geographically, so you can often tour several in one week. If you plan to use state money, apply through Step Up For Students for the Family Empowerment Scholarship or PEP, then ask each school whether it accepts those funds directly or expects you to pay and reimburse. On a visit, ask how many students share a teacher, how they handle different grade levels in one room, and how they report progress to parents. Bring your child along when you can, since fit with the lead teacher tends to predict how a year goes. Get the full cost in writing, including any registration, materials, or activity fees beyond the base rate.
