About this school

PGF Learning Academy is a microschool launching in Montgomery, Alabama, in August 2026 for students in grades three through seven. Serving a cohort of twelve scholars aged nine to fourteen, the institution focuses on addressing foundational academic gaps in reading and core subjects. The program utilizes personalized, mastery-based instruction designed to support student development within a small-group educational setting.

Meet the founder: Mrs. Mottenon

Mrs. Mottenon founded PGF Learning Academy after 22 years in education, including 12 years teaching and 10 years as a Parent Involvement Facilitator. She holds a Life Sciences degree from Indiana State University, a Master’s in Family Therapy, and certifications from Marian and Troy Universities.
