About this school

Res Novae Academy is a microschool located in Pinson, Alabama. The institution utilizes project and inquiry-based learning methodologies to foster core academic skills within a mixed-age group environment. This structural approach distinguishes the school by emphasizing collaborative, student-driven instruction rather than traditional grade-level segregation, catering to families seeking an alternative educational model focused on foundational competencies through interactive study.
