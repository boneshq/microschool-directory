About this school

Legacy Builders Academy is a faith-based microschool located in Gardendale, Alabama, serving students in grades 5 through 10. The institution emphasizes child-led learning and individualized education plans to support personalized academic growth. It prioritizes active parent involvement and community engagement to create a supportive environment that nurtures each student's unique potential while maintaining equitable access to tailored educational resources.

Meet the founder: Yalonda Chandler

Yalonda Chandler, a former teacher with over a decade of experience, co-founded Legacy Builders Academy and serves as its executive director. She also co-founded Black Homeschoolers of Birmingham, leveraging her extensive homeschooling background to support neurodivergent students and the broader community.
