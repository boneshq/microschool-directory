About this school

Wonderstead is a microschool serving kindergarten through eighth-grade students in Birmingham, Alabama. The institution emphasizes a low-pressure educational environment that prioritizes individual curiosity and strong interpersonal relationships. Its daily schedule incorporates quiet study periods, hands-on projects, and outdoor activities within a naturalistic setting. This approach aims to provide a warm, structured alternative to traditional high-pressure schooling by focusing on holistic student development and intentional pacing.

Meet the founder: Carla Barrentine

Carla Barrentine founded Wonderstead, bringing thirteen years of private education and community building experience. With backgrounds in psychology, neuroscience, yoga, and postpartum care, she currently works for a local startup focused on flexible childcare.
