Microschooling & school choice in South Dakota

The directory currently tracks two microschools in South Dakota, and both sit in Sioux Falls. One runs a learner-driven model and the other operates as a microschool, so the mix is even across the two approaches. Tuition is set by each program, and the directory does not publish a fixed price, so families should request current rates directly. For funding, South Dakota does not run a personal-use education savings account. Its Partners in Education Tax Credit Program instead channels insurance-company tax credits into private-school scholarships, so eligibility and award sizes depend on the scholarship granting organization rather than the family applying alone.

Finding the right fit in South Dakota

Sioux Falls is the place to begin, since both of South Dakota's listed microschools operate there. The Pelican School and Acton Academy Sioux Falls give you two different formats to compare, one structured as a microschool and one as a learner-driven program. Reach out to each for current tuition, since the directory does not list a set figure and prices change year to year. On funding, ask whether the school works with a scholarship granting organization under the Partners in Education Tax Credit Program, and check the South Dakota Department of Education for current eligibility rules. When you visit, ask about group size, daily schedule, how progress gets reported to parents, and how the school handles different ages in one room.

South Dakota microschool FAQ

How many microschools are in South Dakota?

The directory lists two microschools in South Dakota, both in Sioux Falls. One uses a learner-driven model and the other runs as a microschool. The directory grows as new programs are added, so check back for fresh listings.

How much does a microschool cost in South Dakota?

Cost depends on the individual program. The directory does not publish a set tuition figure for South Dakota, so contact The Pelican School or Acton Academy Sioux Falls directly for current rates. Many small programs also discuss payment plans during enrollment.

Can you use an ESA or scholarship for a microschool in South Dakota?

South Dakota does not offer a personal education savings account. It does run the Partners in Education Tax Credit Program, which funds private-school scholarships through insurance-company tax credits awarded by scholarship granting organizations. Ask each school whether it partners with one of those organizations.

What types of microschools are in South Dakota?

Both listed programs fall under the alternative-education umbrella. One is a learner-driven school and the other is a microschool, and both keep enrollment small compared with a typical district classroom.

Which South Dakota cities have microschools?

Acton Academy Sioux Falls is the learner-driven option, while The Pelican School is the microschool. Both are in Sioux Falls, which is currently the only South Dakota city with a listed microschool in this directory. Families elsewhere in the state may need to travel or consider homeschooling alongside a co-op.
