About this school

Aim Academy is a microschool located in Sandy, Utah, serving students from Pre-K through sixth grade. This institution offers a small-class environment designed to provide individualized attention and flexible learning paths for its young student body. By maintaining low student-to-teacher ratios, Aim Academy focuses on creating a supportive educational setting that adapts to the specific developmental needs of children in these early academic years.
