Microschooling & school choice in Utah

This directory lists nine microschools and homeschool co-ops in Utah. Learning models among these listings include learner-driven, microschool, and Montessori approaches. Tuition ranges from $5100 to $18000 per year. The Utah Fits All Scholarship is valued at $8000 per student for 2025-26. Homeschooled students receive $4000 for ages five through eleven or $6000 for ages twelve through eighteen. Utah Fits All awarded its first 10000 scholarships in 2024, with homeschooled students making up roughly 80 percent of recipients. Families may be able to use the scholarship for eligible expenses and should confirm with the school.
