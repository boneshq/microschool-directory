Microschooling & school choice in Utah

The directory covers nine schools spread across nine Utah cities, so most towns hold a single listing rather than a cluster. Six of the nine follow a learner-driven model, two run as independent microschools, and one is Montessori. Listed tuition runs from $5,100 to $7,000 a year. Salt Lake City is home to Pinyon Montessori School, while Logan hosts Acton Academy Cache Valley. Utah's Utah Fits All Scholarship gives eligible families an education savings account they can spend on tuition, curriculum, and other approved costs, which can offset much of that tuition range.

Finding the right fit in Utah

Salt Lake City and the surrounding Wasatch Front hold most of the activity, so start there or in Logan and St. George if you live farther out. Apply for the Utah Fits All Scholarship through the state program; approved families get an education savings account to put toward tuition and other eligible expenses, though funds are limited and awarded by lottery in high-demand years. Confirm with each school whether it accepts scholarship payments before you enroll. On a visit, ask how many students share a classroom, how the school handles different grade levels at once, and what a typical day looks like. Request to see the space during school hours so you can watch the learner-driven approach in practice.

Utah microschool FAQ

How many microschools are in Utah?

This directory lists nine microschools and homeschool co-ops across Utah, spread over nine cities. Most are learner-driven academies, with a smaller number of independent microschools and one Montessori program. Listings reach from Salt Lake City and Logan in the north down to Utah's southwest corner.

How much does a microschool cost in Utah?

Listed tuition for Utah microschools runs from $5,100 to $7,000 per year. Costs vary with the model, grade levels, and full-time versus part-time enrollment, so confirm the current rate and what it covers with each school.

Can you use a Utah ESA or scholarship for a microschool?

Yes. Utah runs the Utah Fits All Scholarship, an education savings account program that eligible families can use toward private tuition, curriculum, and other approved expenses. Demand has outpaced funding, so awards are limited and you should apply early and confirm a school accepts the scholarship.

What types of microschools are in Utah?

The nine listings break down into six learner-driven schools, two microschools, and one Montessori program. Several of the learner-driven sites are Acton Academy affiliates, including Acton Academy Cache Valley in Logan.

Where in Utah are these schools located?

They sit across nine cities, from Salt Lake City, Draper, and Woods Cross along the Wasatch Front to Logan in the north. Because each city carries a single listing, your nearest option may be a short drive away.
