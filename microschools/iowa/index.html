Iowa microschool FAQ

How many microschools are in Iowa?

The directory currently lists one microschool in Iowa, Atlas Learning Collective in Marion County. That number reflects schools we have verified, not every program operating statewide, so it will change as more schools are added. How much does a microschool cost in Iowa?

Tuition is not posted for the Iowa listing, so there is no single price to quote. Microschool costs in other states commonly run a few thousand dollars per year up through low five figures, depending on hours and grade level. Ask Atlas Learning Collective directly for current rates and any sibling discounts.

Can I use an ESA or scholarship for a microschool in Iowa?

Yes. Iowa's Students First Education Savings Account opened to all families for the 2025-2026 school year, and parents can apply the funds toward private tuition and approved education expenses. Confirm with each school whether it accepts ESA payments and check the state portal for current award amounts and deadlines.

What types of microschools are in Iowa?

The one Iowa listing uses a project-based model, where students learn through hands-on units and longer assignments instead of a fixed lecture format. Other common formats include Montessori, classic homeschool co-ops, and mixed-age one-room settings, and you may find these among Iowa private schools as the directory grows.

Where in Iowa can I find a microschool?

Marion County sits in central Iowa, south of Des Moines, so families in the Des Moines metro are within reach of Atlas Learning Collective. If you are farther out, search nearby towns and ask private schools or co-ops whether they run small-group, microschool-style classes.
