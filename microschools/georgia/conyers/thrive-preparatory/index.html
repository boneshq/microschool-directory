About this school

Thrive Preparatory Academy is a microschool in Conyers, Georgia, serving students in grades 3 through 12. The institution utilizes a proprietary three-step teaching process designed to help students take ownership of their learning. Additionally, the school provides support for homeschool families through in-person and online tutoring, private lessons, and summer camps. Thrive also partners with Eastside Academic Studies to offer classes in Tucker, Georgia.
