About this school

Rays of Hidden Light Academy is a hybrid microschool in Douglasville, Georgia, serving grades six through twelve. The institution specializes in supporting neurodivergent learners through flexible enrollment options that blend in-person and virtual instruction. Its distinct Creative Pods program allows students to explore diverse interests including art, podcasting, filmmaking, pottery, writing, music, coding, and clothing design. This approach aims to help students develop real-world skills while receiving personalized academic support in a smaller educational setting.

Meet the founder: With

Latoya Moore founded Rays of Hidden Light Academy, drawing on nearly two decades of experience as a classroom educator and academic coach to support diverse learners and improve instructional outcomes.
