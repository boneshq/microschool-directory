About this school

Aya Akoma Academy is a microschool serving grades six through twelve in Decatur County, Georgia, opening in August 2026. The institution emphasizes culturally responsive instruction grounded in Afrocentric perspectives, focusing on endurance and patience. It provides individualized math support and collaborative learning within small class settings. This approach aims to develop critical thinking and real-world problem-solving skills while fostering student identity and academic confidence.

Meet the founder: Bethaney Wright

Bethaney Wright founded Aya Akoma Academy. She is a former sixth-grade math teacher and SAT instructor holding a Master of Arts in Education with a focus on mathematics and social studies.
