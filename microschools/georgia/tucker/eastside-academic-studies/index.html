About this school

Eastside Academic Studies in Tucker, Georgia, serves homeschooling students in grades 7-12 through a la carte microschool classes. The program offers high school-level courses taught by state-certified educators with extensive experience. Distinctive features include hands-on, lab-based science instruction and flexible class durations ranging from 1.5 to 3 hours. The curriculum meets standards recognized by the Georgia Accrediting Commission, providing academic support within a welcoming environment for diverse families.
