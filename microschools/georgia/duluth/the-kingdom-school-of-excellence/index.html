About this school

The Kingdom School of Excellence is a full-time microschool in Duluth, Georgia, serving students in grades two through five. The program offers personalized academic pathways allowing learners to master core subjects at their own pace within a mixed-age environment. Daily activities include Scripture-based goal setting and participation in an Innovation Lab where students develop entrepreneurship skills by transforming creative ideas into real-world business concepts.
