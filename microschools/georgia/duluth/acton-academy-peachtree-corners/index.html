About this school

Acton Academy Peachtree Corners serves students in grades K through 7th grade in Peachtree Corners, Georgia. The microschool utilizes a learner-driven model that allows children’s natural curiosity to guide their educational path. The curriculum emphasizes core academic skills, including reading, writing, and mathematics, alongside historical and scientific studies. Students are provided with meaningful work options and respect for their individual interests within the learning environment.
