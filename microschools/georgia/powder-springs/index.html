EPIC Academy of Excellence

📍 Powder Springs, GA
Project-Based

EPIC Academy of Excellence is a virtual microschool in Powder Springs, Georgia, serving grades 6 through 12. Founded by educator Andrea Hall, it offers individualized instruction and project-based learning within small, mixed-age groups of five to ten students. The curriculum emphasizes critical thinking and self-paced growth. Rooted in a faith-centered foundation, the school focuses on developing leadership, integrity, collaboration, and service alongside academic skills for its enrolled learners.
