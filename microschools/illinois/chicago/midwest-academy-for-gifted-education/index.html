About this school

Midwest Academy for Gifted Education serves students from PreK through 12th grade in Chicago, Illinois. The school specializes in educational support for highly and profoundly gifted individuals. Its curriculum emphasizes tailored programs designed to challenge intellectual curiosity while fostering emotional and social growth. This approach aims to help exceptional learners achieve academic excellence and reach their fullest potential across all developmental areas.
