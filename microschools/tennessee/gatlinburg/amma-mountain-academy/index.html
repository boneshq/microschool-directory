About this school

Amma Mountain Academy is a hybrid microschool in Gatlinburg, Tennessee, serving grades K through 8. The institution utilizes a split-day model featuring morning academics and afternoon project-based enrichment. Students engage with local artisans and receive structured support for homeschooling families through both group and individualized instruction. The program emphasizes holistic development by integrating social-emotional learning with academic goals, offering flexible part-time or full-time enrollment options to accommodate diverse scheduling needs.

Meet the founder: Amanda Mc

Amanda McKay founded Amma Mountain Academy in Gatlinburg, Tennessee. A certified educator with over a decade of experience, she holds a BA in Interdisciplinary Studies K-8 and an MA in Secondary Education with a 6-12 English Language Arts endorsement.
