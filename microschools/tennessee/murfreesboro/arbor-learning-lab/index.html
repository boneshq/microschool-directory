About this school

Arbor Learning Lab serves students in grades 6-12 within Murfreesboro, Tennessee. This microschool emphasizes personalized education through project-based learning and agricultural experiences. The program focuses on building community connections and fostering belonging among its student body. By integrating real-world applications with academic instruction, the school aims to support both personal and scholarly growth for learners in a culturally responsive environment.

Meet the founder: Genevieve

Genevieve, a former high school and charter school founding principal, established Arbor Learning Lab in Rutherford County, Tennessee. Originally from the East Coast, she now resides on a mini farm near Nashville, leveraging her extensive school leadership experience to provide inclusive, student-centered education for local families.
