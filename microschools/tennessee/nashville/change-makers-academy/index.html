About this school

Change Makers Academy operates as a microschool in Nashville, Tennessee, serving students from kindergarten through fifth grade. The program facilitates small-group learning with a licensed teacher, functioning similarly to homeschooling support. The weekly schedule features core academics in the morning and hands-on enrichment activities such as STEAM, entrepreneurship, and crafting in the afternoon. Classes meet Monday through Thursday, offering flexible two- or four-day options for families seeking structured peer-based education.

Meet the founder: Ariel

Ariel founded Change Makers Academy in Nashville, Tennessee. A licensed teacher with nine years of experience, she previously taught grades 1–3 science, served as an interventionist, led a K–6 microschool, and homeschooled neurodiverse students.
