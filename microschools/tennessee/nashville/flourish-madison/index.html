About this school

Flourish Madison is a microschool located in Nashville, Tennessee, serving students in grades six through eight. The program emphasizes developing specific skills, habits, and mindsets referred to as SuperPowers, including agency, curiosity, leadership, and persistence. Students engage in project-based learning called Pursuits designed to foster self-confidence and prepare them for navigating complex environments while cultivating the ability to learn independently.

Meet the founder: John

John co-founded Flourish Madison. An engineer and educator, he previously led Rocketship Public Schools and NetGravity. He holds degrees from Stanford and Vanderbilt University and is a Stanford DCI Fellow, Aspen Institute Crown Fellow, and Ashoka Fellow.
