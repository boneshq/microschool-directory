Change Makers Academy

📍 Nashville, TN
Project-Based

Change Makers Academy operates as a microschool in Nashville, Tennessee, serving students from kindergarten through fifth grade. The program facilitates small-group learning with a licensed teacher, functioning similarly to homeschooling support. The weekly schedule features core academics in the morning and hands-on enrichment activities such as STEAM, entrepreneurship, and crafting in the afternoon. Classes meet Monday through Thursday, offering flexible two- or four-day options for families seeking structured peer-based education.

Dream Builders Learning Center

📍 Nashville, TN
Project-Based

Dream Builders Learning Center serves grades K through 5th in Nashville, Tennessee. This microschool utilizes a whole-child approach to provide academic support and social-emotional well-being within an affirming environment. The curriculum features project-based learning opportunities including computer science with AI and VR, as well as entrepreneurship and financial literacy. The program aims to build student confidence, strengthen critical thinking skills, and promote social justice.

Flourish Madison

📍 Nashville, TN
Project-Based

Flourish Madison is a microschool located in Nashville, Tennessee, serving students in grades six through eight. The program emphasizes developing specific skills, habits, and mindsets referred to as SuperPowers, including agency, curiosity, leadership, and persistence. Students engage in project-based learning called Pursuits designed to foster self-confidence and prepare them for navigating complex environments while cultivating the ability to learn independently.

What types of microschools are in Nashville?

The Nashville listings include Project-Based, Montessori programs. A microschool is a small, independent school (usually 5–40 students across mixed ages); a homeschool co-op is a group of families who share teaching. See the glossary for every model explained.
