Microschools & co-ops in Memphis, TN

Four microschools and learning co-ops serve families in Memphis, Tennessee. Most run as small independent microschools, with one project-based program among the listings. ME Institute

📍 Memphis, TN
Project-Based

The ME Institute in Memphis, Tennessee, serves students from Kindergarten through 7th Grade. This microschool offers career-driven, student-centered programs that emphasize real-world learning experiences. The institution provides personalized instruction tailored to individual strengths and needs. Additionally, the curriculum integrates faith-based teachings to explore the connection between faith, culture, and human development, aiming to help students discover their purpose and navigate life with confidence.

Rhythms of Home Learning Community

📍 Memphis, TN
Microschool

Rhythms of Home Learning Community is a nature-inspired hybrid school in Cordova, Tennessee, serving students from kindergarten through twelfth grade. Founded by an experienced educator, the program offers a flexible alternative to traditional schooling by combining structured in-person instruction on Mondays through Thursdays with independent at-home learning on Fridays. This approach prioritizes individual pacing and curiosity, allowing families to integrate rest, family time, and enrichment into the weekly schedule while maintaining academic engagement.

The FOCUS Academy

📍 Memphis, TN
Microschool

The FOCUS Academy in Memphis, Tennessee, is a microschool for sixth through eighth-grade boys who are twice-exceptional, meaning they are both gifted and neurodivergent. The program emphasizes flexible opportunities for creative and unique students to explore personal interests while developing strengths. It operates as a collaborative learning environment that combines the autonomy of homeschooling with the social dynamics of traditional peer groups.

The Lab School of Memphis

📍 Memphis, TN
Microschool

The Lab School of Memphis serves students in grades K-8 within Memphis, Tennessee. This microschool offers a personalized curriculum designed to integrate real-world experiences into daily learning. The program focuses on developing practical skills through hands-on activities and community engagement, providing an educational environment that emphasizes applied knowledge alongside traditional academic instruction for its young learners.

Microschooling & school choice in Memphis

This directory lists four microschools and homeschool co-ops in Memphis. Three are general microschools and one follows a project-based approach, so the options stay fairly close in format. Tennessee passed the Education Freedom Scholarship Act in 2025, creating a statewide education savings account that families can apply toward private and alternative schooling. Eligibility and award amounts are set by the state, and not every school participates. Parents should ask each program directly whether it accepts the scholarship before counting on those funds.

Finding the right fit in Memphis

Start by looking at the four Memphis listings side by side: class size, daily schedule, and whether the program is a general microschool or the project-based option. Contact two or three that fit your child and ask about current openings, since small programs fill quickly. The Tennessee Education Freedom Scholarship Act funds an education savings account, but you need to confirm both your eligibility with the state and whether a given school accepts it. On a visit, ask how teachers track progress, what a typical day looks like, and how they handle children at different levels. Bring your own questions about discipline, communication with parents, and what happens if the fit is wrong partway through the year.

Memphis microschool FAQ

How many microschools are in Memphis?

This directory currently lists four microschools and homeschool co-ops in Memphis. Three operate as general microschools, including The Lab School of Memphis and The FOCUS Academy, and one runs a project-based model. The number changes as new programs open, so check back for additions.

How much does a microschool in Memphis cost?

Tuition for the four Memphis programs varies by school and is set individually rather than by a single fixed rate. Class size, the number of days per week, and any included materials all affect the price, so ask each program for its current schedule and fees. Some accept the state education savings account, which can lower out-of-pocket cost.

What types of microschools are available in Memphis?

The four Memphis listings include general microschools and one project-based program. ME Institute is the project-based option, while the others run as small mixed-age or grouped microschools. These settings keep enrollment low and adjust pace to the individual student.

Can I use a Tennessee ESA or scholarship for a Memphis microschool?

Tennessee passed the Education Freedom Scholarship Act in 2025, which created a statewide education savings account for private and alternative schooling. Whether you can use it depends on state eligibility rules and on whether the specific school participates. Apply through the state program and confirm acceptance with the school before you enroll.

How do I enroll my child in a Memphis microschool?

To enroll in a Memphis microschool, reach out to the program directly, since most handle admissions on a rolling basis rather than through a central system. Ask about open seats for the grade you need, any assessment or trial day, and the deposit timeline. Rhythms of Home Learning Community and the other listed programs can walk you through their own steps.
