ME Institute

📍 Memphis, TN
Project-Based

The ME Institute in Memphis, Tennessee, serves students from Kindergarten through 7th Grade. This microschool offers career-driven, student-centered programs that emphasize real-world learning experiences. The institution provides personalized instruction tailored to individual strengths and needs. Additionally, the curriculum integrates faith-based teachings to explore the connection between faith, culture, and human development, aiming to help students discover their purpose and navigate life with confidence.

Rhythms of Home Learning Community

📍 Memphis, TN
Microschool

Rhythms of Home Learning Community is a nature-inspired hybrid school in Cordova, Tennessee, serving students from kindergarten through twelfth grade. Founded by an experienced educator, the program offers a flexible alternative to traditional schooling by combining structured in-person instruction on Mondays through Thursdays with independent at-home learning on Fridays. This approach prioritizes individual pacing and curiosity, allowing families to integrate rest, family time, and enrichment into the weekly schedule while maintaining academic engagement.

The FOCUS Academy

📍 Memphis, TN
Microschool

The FOCUS Academy in Memphis, Tennessee, is a microschool for sixth through eighth-grade boys who are twice-exceptional, meaning they are both gifted and neurodivergent. The program emphasizes flexible opportunities for creative and unique students to explore personal interests while developing strengths. It operates as a collaborative learning environment that combines the autonomy of homeschooling with the social dynamics of traditional peer groups.
