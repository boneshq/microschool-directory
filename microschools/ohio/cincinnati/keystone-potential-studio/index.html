About this school

Keystone Potential Studio serves third through fifth graders in Cincinnati, Ohio. The microschool utilizes a mastery-based curriculum that allows students to progress at their own pace. Instruction combines personalized academic guidance with independent study and hands-on creative projects. The program emphasizes social-emotional development and real-world application, fostering student ownership of learning. This approach aims to build confidence and support individualized growth within a flexible educational framework.

Meet the founder: Dena Combs

Dena Combs founded Keystone Potential Studio. She brings over ten years of experience as an educator, math coach, and Assistant Principal of Instruction to the microschool.
