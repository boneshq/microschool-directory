Microschooling & school choice in Ohio

Ohio hosts 17 microschools and homeschool co-ops offering Montessori, learner-driven, project-based, and microschool models. Tuition for these listings ranges from $9,790 to $9,790 annually. Families may access the state EdChoice Expansion voucher, which has no enrollment cap. The program provides up to $6,166 for K-8 students and $8,408 for grades 9-12 during the 2025-26 school year. Full awards apply to households earning under 450% of the federal poverty level. Recipients should confirm eligibility with individual schools for specific expense coverage.
