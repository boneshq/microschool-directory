About this school

The Kairos Workshop is a microschool in Youngstown, Ohio, serving students in grades 6th through 9th. The curriculum emphasizes Arts, Science, and Leadership development. Instruction focuses on strengthening academic abilities through debate, writing, public speaking, artistic expression, and active scientific engagement. The program aims to cultivate communication skills, critical thinking, and confidence, encouraging students to strive toward their full potential within a collaborative educational environment.

Meet the founder

Rahman Kevin Smith founded The Kairos Workshop. He spent twenty years teaching at Shenzhen University and serving as Director of the Self-Access Language Learning Center at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, while founding debate programs and writing curriculum.
