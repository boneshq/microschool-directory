About this school

Pioneer Reading Center is a microschool located in Wayne, Ohio, specializing in personalized instruction for neurodiverse students. The program focuses specifically on reading education and supports families who are expecting or actively seeking tailored academic environments. This specialized approach aims to meet the unique learning needs of its student population through individualized educational strategies rather than traditional classroom models.
