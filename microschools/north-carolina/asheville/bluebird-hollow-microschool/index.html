About this school

Bluebird Hollow is a learner-driven microschool in West Asheville for ages 5-12. Mornings are core skills (math, reading, writing) in mixed-age studios; afternoons are project blocks — woodworking, creek ecology, and a spring market where kids run real stalls. Two guides, 16 learners max. UPDATED BY OWNER: Fall 2026 enrollment is now open with 4 spots remaining.
