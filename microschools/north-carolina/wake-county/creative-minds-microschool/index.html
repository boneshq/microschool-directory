About this school

Creative Minds Microschool in Wake County, North Carolina, serves students from Kindergarten through eighth grade. The institution utilizes a personalized learning model within a small group environment. Curriculum is tailored to individual student needs, incorporating project-based activities and specialized instruction. This approach aims to foster independent study habits and creative problem-solving skills among its diverse student body.

Meet the founder: Dr. Lisa

Dr. Lisa Swinson founded Creative Minds Microschool. A National Board Certified teacher with 27 years of experience, she served as a curriculum director, principal, and state education consultant before establishing the school.
