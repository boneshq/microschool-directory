About this school

Wellspring Preparatory School serves students in grades K through 5th Grade in Monroe, North Carolina. As Union County’s first faith-based STEM microschool, it emphasizes academic rigor within a religious framework. The institution is founded by a National Board Certified Teacher with over thirty years of elementary education experience. Its distinct approach combines science, technology, engineering, and mathematics instruction with character development to provide an intimate learning environment for young learners.

Meet the founder: Stephanie Van

Stephanie Van Horn is a National Board Certified Teacher (three times) with over 31 years of classroom experience. She founded Wellspring Preparatory School, Union County’s first microschool, to provide personalized academic and spiritual support for students overlooked by traditional systems.
