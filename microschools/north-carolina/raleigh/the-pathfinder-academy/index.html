About this school

The Pathfinder Academy is a full-time private high school in Raleigh, North Carolina, serving ninth through twelfth graders. Founded by veteran physics educator Stacey Kaufman, the institution emphasizes inquiry and project-based learning to promote critical thinking. Students engage in hands-on problem solving and authentic experiences within a collaborative community designed to foster independent thought and self-direction, preparing graduates for life beyond high school.

Meet the founder: Stacey Kaufman

Stacey Kaufman founded The Pathfinder Academy. She holds a B.S. in Physics and an M.S. in Secondary Science Education from the University of Tennessee. A veteran educator with nearly two decades of experience, she previously served as Science Department Head at Research Triangle High School.
