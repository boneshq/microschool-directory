About this school

Opening in the New Bern area for the 2027–28 school year (grades 5–8). At the heart of Cypress Learning Studio is a simple purpose: restoring joy and humanity in education so young people feel known, supported, and excited to learn. Cypress Learning Studio combines the personalization of homeschooling with the structure, friendships, and shared experiences of a small school community. Strong relationships help us understand each learner’s strengths, interests, and needs, while our Mind–Body–Soul approach supports academic, social, and emotional growth.
