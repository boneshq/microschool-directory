About this school

Fireside Academy serves students in grades one through six in Colorado City, Arizona, supporting parents who choose homeschooling or blended schooling models. The school integrates Montessori materials with educational philosophies from Rudolf Steiner and Charlotte Mason to create individualized learning experiences. Daily activities emphasize connection with nature and community through gardening, animal care, and environmental stewardship, focusing on holistic development within a microschool setting.
