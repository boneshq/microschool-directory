About this school

Grace Place Learning Lab is a faith-based microschool serving students in grades K through 5th Grade in Tempe, Arizona. The institution utilizes small class sizes and low student-to-teacher ratios to provide personalized instruction and hands-on learning experiences. Its curriculum integrates Bible principles with STEM, literacy, arts, and social-emotional development. The school emphasizes multicultural perspectives, experiential learning, and strong parent partnerships to support diverse educational needs within a collaborative community environment.

Meet the founder: Hi

Briannah holds a bachelor’s degree in child and family studies and has ten years of public education experience. She founded Grace Place Learning Lab to provide a faith-affirming educational environment for children and families.
