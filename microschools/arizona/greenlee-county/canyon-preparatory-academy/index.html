About this school

Canyon Preparatory Academy is a 100% online college preparatory program located in Litchfield Park, Arizona, serving students in grades 10th through 12th. The school offers Standard and Honors pathways, including faith-based options, for both full-time and part-time enrollment. Instruction covers core academics, sciences, humanities, and electives, alongside certificate programs, delivered by educators with specialized backgrounds in biology, medicine, psychology, and curriculum development.
