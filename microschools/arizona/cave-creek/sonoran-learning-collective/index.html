About this school

Sonoran Learning Collective serves students in grades four through eight in Cave Creek, Arizona. As a Christian hybrid microschool, it requires on-campus attendance two days per week while the remaining coursework is completed at home with parental oversight. The curriculum emphasizes a Biblical worldview and integrates classical education principles. Core subjects include reading, writing, mathematics, critical thinking, and character formation within a screen-free environment.

Meet the founder: Marta Mac

Marta MacBan, a Phoenix native with a bachelor’s degree in mass communication and Spanish from Arizona State University, founded Sonoran Learning Collective. Her background includes developing a cottage school and advocating for school choice through local nonprofit work.
