About this school

Faithscape Learning Pod serves students in grades three through eight within Casa Grande, Arizona. The program emphasizes literacy, creative expression, and character development through small class sizes that allow for personalized academic attention. It integrates a supportive Christian environment with engaging learning experiences designed to foster curiosity and social skills while encouraging family involvement in the educational process.

Meet the founder: Deja Hillis

Deja Hillis founded Faithscape Learning Pod in Casa Grande, AZ. She holds an English degree with a literature certificate from Northern Arizona University and has worked as a K-2 writing teacher, lead kindergarten teacher, children’s book author, freelance educational writer, and tutor.
