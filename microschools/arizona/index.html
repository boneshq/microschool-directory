Microschooling & school choice in Arizona

Arizona lists 20 microschools and homeschool co-ops, with Queen Creek and Tucson serving as the largest hubs. Tuition among these schools ranges from $3200 to $7500 per year. Learning models include Microschool, Project-Based, Learner-Driven, and Montessori options. The state offers a universal Empowerment Scholarship Account (ESA) for all K-12 students since 2022. This program served more than 100,000 students in 2025-26. Most awards fall between about $7,000 and $8,000 per student. Funds cover private tuition, homeschool curricula, tutoring, and other approved expenses.
