About this school

Velos Collective operates in Taylors, South Carolina, serving students from kindergarten through twelfth grade. This microschool convenes twice weekly on Tuesdays and Thursdays, providing a flexible, à la carte curriculum focused on life skills and select academic subjects. Participants may register for individual or multiple classes to customize their educational experience. The program emphasizes holistic development and character formation for its diverse student body.
