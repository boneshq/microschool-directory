About this school

A Home For School is a microschool located in Moncks Corner, South Carolina, serving students from kindergarten through eighth grade. The institution utilizes a self-paced educational model designed to honor the unique cultural backgrounds of its student body. This approach prioritizes individual learning rhythms and fosters a supportive atmosphere where learners progress at their own speed within a community-focused environment.

Meet the founder: Isis Spann

Isis Spann, a first-generation college graduate with a master’s from Grand Canyon University and a doctorate in progress, founded A Home for School. She previously taught elementary education in South Carolina for twelve years and serves as CEO of FUNdamentals of Learning, LLC.
