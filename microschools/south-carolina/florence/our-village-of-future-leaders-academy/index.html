About this school

Our Village of Future Leaders Academy in Florence, South Carolina, serves students from kindergarten through sixth grade. Founded by an educator with over ten years of childcare experience, the microschool addresses concerns regarding declining literacy by utilizing an inquiry- and project-based curriculum. This approach emphasizes hands-on learning to strengthen academic understanding and confidence. The institution aims to build community while remaining accessible to families regardless of their ability to pay tuition.

Meet the founder

Char founded Our Village of Future Leaders Academy, drawing on over ten years in childcare. As a single mother, she applies her educational passion to address declining academic skills while fostering community and holistic child development.
