About this school

Lionsgate Academy serves students in grades one through nine within Aiken County, South Carolina. The institution distinguishes itself by offering personalized learning experiences tailored to individual student needs rather than utilizing a standardized curriculum. This approach emphasizes critical thinking and problem-solving skills alongside academic instruction. Additionally, the school integrates faith-based values into its educational framework while maintaining respect for diverse beliefs and perspectives among its student body.

Meet the founder: Barbara Hancock

Barbara Hancock founded Lionsgate Academy. She holds double Bachelor’s degrees in Education and Music, plus a Master’s from the University of South Carolina. With over 22 years of public and private school experience, she focuses on underserved students.
