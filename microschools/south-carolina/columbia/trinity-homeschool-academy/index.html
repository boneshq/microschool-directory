About this school

Trinity Homeschool Academy operates in Columbia, South Carolina, providing hybrid learning pods for grades K-5 alongside classes for middle and high school students. The institution distinguishes itself by employing teachers who simultaneously homeschool their own children while instructing others, fostering a shared commitment to academic success. This model supports families by partnering with parents who remain accountable through their chosen associations, offering small class sizes and individualized learning goals within a collaborative educational environment.
