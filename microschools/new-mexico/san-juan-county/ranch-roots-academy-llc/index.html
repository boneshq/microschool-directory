About this school

Ranch & Roots Academy is a private K-5 school located in San Juan County, New Mexico. The institution integrates core academic subjects with agricultural education through hands-on experiences such as gardening, livestock care, and farm-to-table nutrition. This model connects reading, writing, math, and science to practical life skills, emphasizing stewardship and character development within its faith-based curriculum for young learners.
