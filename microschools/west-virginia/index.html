Microschooling & school choice in West Virginia

The directory currently shows two West Virginia programs spread across two locations: Martinsburg in the Eastern Panhandle and Wayne County in the southwest. One follows a project-based approach and the other runs as a microschool, so both keep enrollment small and the schedule flexible. Listed schools have not published set tuition, which is common for newer programs that price by grade or by days attended, so you will need to ask each one directly. On the funding side, West Virginia runs the Hope Scholarship, an education savings account that deposits state money into an account families can spend on tuition, curriculum, and other approved costs. Wellbright School in Martinsburg and Hundred Acre Learning Center in Wayne County are the two programs in the listings right now.

Finding the right fit in West Virginia

Start with the two cities that have listings: Martinsburg in the Eastern Panhandle and Wayne County near the Kentucky line. Look up the Hope Scholarship on the state Treasurer's site to check whether your child qualifies and how much the account pays this year, since the deposit amount is set annually. Once approved, that money can go toward tuition and approved learning materials, so confirm with each program that it accepts Hope Scholarship payments before you enroll. When you visit, ask how many students attend, what a typical day looks like, and how the school handles different grade levels in one room. Also ask how they report progress to parents and what they expect families to cover at home.

West Virginia microschool FAQ

How many microschools are in West Virginia?

This directory lists two microschools and homeschool co-ops in West Virginia. They sit in two separate areas, Martinsburg and Wayne County. The count reflects schools that have submitted listings, so it can grow as more programs join.

How much does a microschool cost in West Virginia?

Tuition is not posted for the West Virginia listings, which often means a program prices by grade level or by the number of days a child attends. Contact each school directly for current rates, and ask whether the Hope Scholarship can offset the cost.

Can you use an ESA or scholarship for a microschool in West Virginia?

Yes. West Virginia runs the Hope Scholarship, a state education savings account that deposits funds families can spend on tuition and approved learning expenses. Apply through the state Treasurer's office, then confirm the specific school accepts Hope Scholarship payments.

What types of microschools are in West Virginia?

The two listings use different formats: Wellbright School in Martinsburg follows a project-based model, and Hundred Acre Learning Center in Wayne County runs as a microschool. Both keep groups small and mix ages or grades more than a standard classroom would.

How does the Hope Scholarship award work each year?

The Hope Scholarship sets its deposit amount each year and has been expanding eligibility toward all school-age children in the state. Check the state Treasurer's site for the current year's award and the application window before you commit to a program.
