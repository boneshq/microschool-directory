About this school

Wellbright School is a microschool located in Martinsburg, West Virginia, serving students in grades 2 through 9. The institution distinguishes itself by bridging theoretical knowledge with practical application through comprehensive vocational training and the instillation of practical life skills. Alongside maintaining high academic standards, the school prioritizes character development and the mental and emotional wellbeing of its students to prepare them for diverse life aspects.

Meet the founder: Katerina Belik

Katerina Belik founded Wellbright School. She holds a PhD and possesses certifications in ELA, special education, school counseling, and administration. Her background includes twenty-eight years of teaching experience, leading ELA departments to historic academic highs before launching Vedara.
