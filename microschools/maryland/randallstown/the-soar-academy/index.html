About this school

The SOAR Academy is a microschool located in Randallstown, Maryland, serving students from kindergarten through fifth grade. The institution offers a full-time academic program that integrates core subjects with instruction in entrepreneurship, financial literacy, STEM, leadership development, and physical wellness. Founded by Andréa Harper, M.Ed., the school utilizes an intentional daily schedule to provide a structured learning environment focused on personalized student support and preparation for future leadership roles.

Meet the founder: Andr

Andréa Harper, M.Ed., founded The SOAR Academy after over a decade in public education instructional support, HR, and operations. As an educational leader and mother, she established the school to bridge strong academics with real-world preparation.
