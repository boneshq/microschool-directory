About this school

ReWild Family Academy is a microschool located in Abbyville, Kansas, serving students in grades K through 8. Housed in a renovated one-hundred-year-old church building, the institution blends nature-based learning with individualized educational paths for children ages five to thirteen. The program emphasizes collaborative projects and fosters an appreciation for creation within its Reno County community setting.
