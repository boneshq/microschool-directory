Microschooling & school choice in Wichita

The directory lists 41 schools across Wichita. Microschools make up the bulk of them at 19 programs, followed by 9 faith-based schools, 4 homeschool co-ops, 3 learner-driven programs, and 2 classical schools. Tuition varies widely by program, so contact each school directly for current rates, sibling discounts, and any sliding-scale options. Kansas does not run a statewide education savings account, but it does offer the Tax Credit for Low Income Students Scholarship Program, which funds private-school tuition for eligible students through donor-supported scholarships. Check with the Kansas State Department of Education to confirm current eligibility rules before you count on any funding.

Finding the right fit in Wichita

Start by filtering the 41 Wichita listings by the model that fits your child, whether that is a microschool like Freedom Preparatory, a classical co-op like Apollos Schole Community, or a faith-based program such as Charity Christian Academy. Email two or three schools to ask about open seats, grade levels served, weekly schedule, and what a typical day looks like. If you plan to use scholarship money, ask whether the school accepts students funded through Kansas's tax-credit scholarship, and confirm your eligibility with the Kansas State Department of Education first. On a visit, watch how teachers handle a mixed-age room, ask the student-to-teacher ratio, and request references from current families. Pricing is rarely posted, so get the full cost in writing, including registration fees and any materials charges.

Wichita microschool FAQ

How many microschools are in Wichita?

Our directory currently lists 41 microschools and homeschool co-ops in Wichita. That is the deepest selection in Kansas, spanning independent microschools, faith-based academies, and parent-run co-ops. Listings change as new programs open, so check back for updates.

How much do Wichita microschools cost?

Cost depends heavily on the model and the hours offered, and most Wichita programs do not publish a flat tuition figure. Part-time co-ops tend to cost far less than full-time microschools, and some faith-based schools offer congregation or sibling discounts. Contact each school directly to get the full price in writing before enrolling.

What types of microschools can I find in Wichita?

The Wichita listings break down into 19 microschools, 9 faith-based schools, 4 homeschool co-ops, 3 learner-driven programs, and 2 classical schools. Some schools blend categories, such as Concordia Academy, which combines classical and faith-based instruction. Pick the model that matches how your child learns best.

Can I use a Kansas ESA or scholarship for these schools?

Kansas does not offer a statewide education savings account as of 2026, but it runs the Tax Credit for Low Income Students Scholarship Program, which can cover private-school tuition for qualifying students. Eligibility is income-based and tied to specific schools that accept the scholarship. Confirm the current rules with the Kansas State Department of Education and ask each school whether it participates.

How do I enroll my child at a Wichita microschool?

Most Wichita programs enroll on a rolling basis, though popular ones like Freedom Preparatory and Wonder can fill their seats early. Reach out well before the school year to ask about waitlists and start dates. If a school near you is full, the directory lists nearby options across the Wichita area you can compare.
