California microschool FAQ

How many microschools are in California?

Our directory currently lists 29 microschools and homeschool co-ops across 26 cities in California. The list grows as new programs open and owners register.

What's the difference between a microschool and a homeschool co-op?

A microschool is a small, independent school with its own staff (usually 5–40 students, mixed ages). A homeschool co-op is a group of homeschooling families who share teaching while parents stay in charge of their child's education. This directory lists both — see the glossary for every term explained.

Can I use an ESA or school-choice funds for a microschool in California?

It depends on California's current programs. A growing number of states fund Education Savings Accounts (ESAs), vouchers, or tax-credit scholarships that families can apply toward microschool tuition. Check your state's education agency for current eligibility and approved expenses.
