Microschooling & school choice in California

California hosts 28 microschools and homeschool co-ops, with San Francisco serving as a primary hub. The state recorded approximately 547,561 homeschooled students in September 2024, representing roughly 4.4% of K-12 students. Listed tuition for these institutions ranges from $9,050 to $9,050 annually. Learning models include Learner-Driven, Montessori, Project-Based, and Microschool approaches. California offers no private school choice program, meaning no ESA, voucher, or tax-credit scholarship is available for private or home education.
