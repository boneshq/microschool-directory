MAAT K12 VIRTUAL PRIVATE SCHOOL

📍 Merced, CA
Online school

MAAT K12 is an independent, virtual private K–12 school dedicated to providing personalized, high-quality education in a flexible, supportive online learning environment. Our standards-based, STEM-integrated curriculum combines English Language Arts, Mathematics, Science, History, Technology, and Social-Emotional Learning to prepare students for academic success, college, careers, and lifelong learning. Students benefit from live teacher-led instruction, interactive digital lessons, individualized assignments, academic assessments, and opportunities for enrichment. Our structured learning approach balances scheduled online classes with independent learning, allowing students to progress while developing responsibility, creativity, and critical-thinking skills. We recognize that every child learns differently. Our program supports diverse learning styles, accelerated learners, students who need additional academic assistance, and families seeking an alternative to traditional classroom education. Through our StudentMe learning platform, families can access educational resources and support their children's academic progress. At MAAT K12, we believe technology should expand educational opportunities, not limit them. We strive to create an inclusive, engaging learning community where every student has the opportunity to achieve their full potential.
