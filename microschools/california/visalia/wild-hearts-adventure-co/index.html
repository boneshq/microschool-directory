About this school

Wild Hearts Adventure Co. serves students in grades 4 through 12 in Visalia, California, meeting twice weekly for project-based learning and core skill development. The program utilizes educational platforms like Miacademy and Curiosity Chronicles to support personalized instruction in math, ELA, science, and social studies. A distinct feature is its Quest system, where learners earn badges to showcase achievements across guided and self-directed activities focused on autonomy and individual strengths.
