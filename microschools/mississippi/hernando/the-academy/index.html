About this school

The Academy is a microschool in Hernando, Mississippi, serving students in grades 6 through 10. The program combines multi-age community time with small-group instruction in core subjects, allowing for personalized academic pacing. Students engage in collaborative exploration of interest-based topics, emphasizing emotional intelligence and resilience. This model supports adolescents seeking structured environments that prioritize both personal development and academic achievement within a cohesive, supportive framework.

Meet the founder: Dr. Ny

Dr. Ny G. is a former U.S. Army Officer and JAG Corps acceptee with a doctoral degree in law and policy. A Portland native raised in a military family, she founded The Academy after relocating to Mississippi in 2015 to support her daughter’s learning needs.
