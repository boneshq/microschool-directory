About this school

Harper Learning Academy operates as a microschool for students in grades two through ten within Hinds County, Mississippi. The institution specializes in providing educational support specifically tailored for learners with differences. Its distinct approach focuses on identifying individual student strengths and cultivating their unique gifts to foster a sense of purpose and engagement within the broader global society.
