About this school

Envision Empowerment Academy in Petal, Mississippi, serves grades 6 through 10 as a complex-based hybrid micro-school. The program offers rigorous core academics aligned with state standards alongside optional dual enrollment opportunities for advanced credit. Students design personalized learning pathways supported by mentorship and structured accountability. A distinct feature includes entrepreneurship courses where learners develop viable businesses, reinforcing social-emotional development and leadership skills to prepare students for college readiness and real-world impact.

Meet the founder: Shaft V.

Shaft V. Brown holds a BA in Elementary Education and an MA in Adult Education and Training. With over 20 years of experience as a teacher, curriculum developer, and consultant for Mississippi agencies, he founded Envision Empowerment Academy.
