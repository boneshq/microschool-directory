Empowered Prep Academy

📍 Jackson, MS
Project-Based

Empowered Prep Academy serves students in grades 3 through 12 in Jackson, Mississippi. The microschool employs dedicated teachers and specialists to support diverse learning needs. Its curriculum extends beyond standard academics to empower families and raise awareness regarding issues such as Dyslexia. The program focuses on equipping individuals with practical skills designed to help them thrive personally and make a lasting impact within their local communities.
