About this school

The Learning Connection operates a microschool in DeWitt, Arkansas, serving students in grades K through 5. The institution collaborates with families to support homeschool journeys by establishing personalized academic and personal goals. Classes are tailored to individual needs, allowing learners to progress at their own pace while engaging in hands-on, real-world projects designed to connect educational concepts directly to practical life experiences.

Meet the founder: Hi

Cindy founded The Learning Connection. She holds a Bachelor’s in Computer Science from Arkansas Tech University and a Master’s in Teaching from the University of Arkansas at Monticello, establishing a project-based microschool to support the homeschool community.
