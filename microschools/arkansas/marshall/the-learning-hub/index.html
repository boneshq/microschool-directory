About this school

The Learning Hub is a microschool in Marshall, Arkansas, serving students from kindergarten through eighth grade. Founded by an advocate for personalized learning and neurodiversity, the school focuses on supporting individual educational journeys. It offers a community-oriented environment designed to celebrate and assist each student’s unique development within the local educational landscape.
