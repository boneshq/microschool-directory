Microschooling & school choice in Arkansas

Arkansas hosts seven microschools and homeschool co-ops offering Microschool, Learner-Driven, Montessori, and Project-Based models. Families may use the state’s LEARNS Act Education Freedom Account to cover eligible expenses like private tuition and curriculum. Eligible K-12 students receive about $6,864 in 2025-26, rising to roughly $7,208 in 2026-27 as eligibility expands. Tuition varies by program, so families should ask each school for current rates and confirm with the school regarding funding acceptance. Listings span the state.
