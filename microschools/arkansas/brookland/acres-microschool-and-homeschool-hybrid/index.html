About this school

ACRES Microschool and Homeschool Hybrid in Brookland, Arkansas, serves students from kindergarten through seventh grade. The program emphasizes self-paced, mastery-based instruction in reading and mathematics while incorporating project-based learning opportunities. This approach allows students to explore personal interests and develop strengths, aiming to build a strong academic foundation alongside the ability to positively impact their community and the wider world.
