Microschooling & school choice in District of Columbia

Four microschools and homeschool co-ops operate in the District of Columbia, primarily within Washington hubs. Learning models include Montessori and Microschool approaches. Tuition varies by program; families should ask each school for current rates. Approximately 3.04 percent of DC K-12 students were homeschooled in 2023-24, up from about 0.4 percent in 2019-20. The federally funded D.C. Opportunity Scholarship Program offers income-limited vouchers worth up to about $10,700 for K-8 and $16,000 for grades 9-12, adjusted annually for inflation. Families may be able to use it for eligible expenses and should confirm with the school.
