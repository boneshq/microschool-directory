Microschooling & school choice in Washington

Washington hosts four microschools and homeschool co-ops in this directory. Listings include Montessori and Microschool models. Tuition varies by program; families should ask each school for current rates. The District is among the largest hubs for these schools. Families may use the federally funded D.C. Opportunity Scholarship Program, which offers income-limited vouchers worth up to about $10,700 for K-8 and $16,000 for grades 9-12, adjusted annually for inflation. Eligible expenses vary by program, so families should confirm with the school.
