About this school

AMPed Hybrid Academy serves students from PreK through eighth grade in Farmington Hills, Michigan. This microschool caters to learners who may not fit into traditional educational settings, including those who are advanced for their grade level or neurodivergent. The institution provides a supportive environment designed to address specific academic and social needs that standard schools might overlook.
