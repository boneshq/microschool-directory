Maine microschool FAQ

How many microschools are in Maine?

This directory lists one microschool in Maine right now: Acton Academy Kennebunkport in Kennebunk. The directory also includes homeschool co-ops as more programs register. Because the count is small, families often look at nearby states or online options as well.

How much does a microschool cost in Maine?

Tuition is not posted for the Maine listing, so there is no set figure to quote here. Learner-driven microschools in other states commonly fall in the several-thousand-dollar range per year, but rates vary widely. Contact Acton Academy Kennebunkport directly to get its current pricing and any aid options.

Can I use an ESA or scholarship in Maine?

Maine does not run a private-school ESA, so there is no state account that pays microschool tuition directly. The state does operate a town tuitioning program: students living in towns without their own public school can attend an approved school at public expense, which sometimes includes private schools. Whether a given microschool qualifies depends on its approval status and your town, so verify both with your district.

What models or types of microschools are in Maine?

The single Maine listing follows a learner-driven model. In that format students set personal goals, work at their own pace, and take more ownership of how they spend the day than in a standard classroom.

Where in Maine can I find a microschool to visit?

Acton Academy Kennebunkport in Kennebunk is the one Maine listing at the moment. Kennebunk sits in York County in the southern coastal part of the state, within driving distance of the Portland area, which gives families in southern Maine a reachable option to tour.
