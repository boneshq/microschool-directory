About this school

Unique Minds Unite is a microschool in Haiku, Hawaii, serving students in grades 6 through 8. The program focuses on early adolescents by emphasizing individuality, curiosity, and meaningful learning experiences. Classes typically consist of 11 to 14-year-olds who develop social-emotional, identity, and team-building skills. The curriculum is collaborative, with students designing their own educational paths as the school year progresses through various phases that shift daily rhythms while maintaining a consistent goal of creating an inclusive environment for all learners.

Meet the founder: Hi

Zaneta Papis founded Unique Minds Unite after twenty years in tech and raising a uniquely minded child, leveraging her background in innovation to create an afterschool program focused on creativity and problem-solving.
