Saint Louis Sudbury School

📍 St. Louis, MO

Saint Louis Sudbury School is located in St. Louis, Missouri, serving students across all grade levels through self-directed education. The institution operates under a democratic governance model where youth and adults share equal authority over school operations. By eliminating mandatory curricula, homework, grades, and standardized testing, the school provides an environment focused on personal interest exploration and community development rather than traditional academic metrics.

Finding the right fit in St. Louis

Start by reading the three St. Louis listings and noting which model fits your child: Montessori, Sudbury-style, or learner-driven. Each approach handles structure and pacing differently, so a short list of priorities helps you compare them fairly. For funding, look into MOScholars; the tax-credit scholarship reimburses qualified education expenses for eligible Missouri families, and you apply through an approved educational assistance organization. Contact each school early, since microschools often cap enrollment at small numbers and fill spots before the school year. On a visit, ask about group size, how teachers track progress, the daily schedule, and what a typical week looks like. Request to observe a class in session rather than relying on a tour alone.

How many microschools are in St. Louis?

What does a microschool in St. Louis typically cost?

Costs are set by each school and depend on the model, the child's age, and whether the program runs full or part time. The directory does not publish a fixed tuition figure for these St. Louis schools, so request a current rate sheet from each one. What models or types of microschools are available here?

The three listings split across Montessori, Sudbury-style microschooling, and a learner-driven approach. Montessori uses mixed-age classrooms and hands-on materials; Sudbury models give students wide say over how they spend their day; learner-driven programs build around individual goals and pacing. Visiting each one helps you see how the philosophy plays out day to day.

Can I use a Missouri scholarship or ESA for a microschool?

Yes. Missouri offers MOScholars, a tax-credit scholarship that can offset private and microschool costs for eligible families. You apply through an approved educational assistance organization, and eligibility depends on income and other household factors. Confirm the current rules and deadlines before you count on a specific award.

How do I enroll my child in a St. Louis microschool?

Reach out to schools like Saint Louis Sudbury School or Guidepost Montessori at Central West End directly to ask about openings. Microschools keep classes small, so seats can go quickly once enrollment opens. Ask about waitlists and start dates, and whether mid-year entry is possible if you miss the main cycle.
