Microschooling & school choice in Missouri

The directory covers eight schools spread across six Missouri cities, with St. Louis holding the largest cluster and listings also reaching Kansas City, Liberty, Ballwin, and Creve Coeur. Reported tuition sits around $14,800 a year, though individual programs set their own rates and some offer sliding scales. The model mix is balanced: three Montessori programs, three learner-driven schools, and two that describe themselves simply as microschools. Guidepost Montessori runs two of the listed campuses, while learner-driven options include Origins KC and Wonder KC. On the funding side, Missouri runs MOScholars, an Empowerment Scholarship Accounts program backed by tax-credit donations and administered through the State Treasurer's office, which can offset private tuition for qualifying families.

Finding the right fit in Missouri

Start your search in the metro areas where listings concentrate: St. Louis and its western suburbs like Ballwin and Creve Coeur, or the Kansas City side near Liberty. Look into MOScholars before you commit, since that program funds Empowerment Scholarship Accounts that families can put toward tuition at participating schools; eligibility and award amounts are set by the State Treasurer's office, so check the current rules there. When you tour a school, ask how mixed-age grouping works day to day, what the student-to-teacher ratio actually is, and how the staff tracks academic progress without a standard report card. Request the full tuition schedule in writing, including any registration or materials fees beyond the headline number. A short visit during regular class hours tells you more than any brochure.

Missouri microschool FAQ

How many microschools are in Missouri?

This directory lists eight microschools and homeschool co-ops in Missouri, spread across six cities. St. Louis has the most options, with additional listings in Kansas City, Liberty, Ballwin, and Creve Coeur. The schools cover Montessori, learner-driven, and general microschool approaches.

How much does a microschool cost in Missouri?

Listed tuition runs around $14,800 per year, though each school sets its own price and some publish lower rates or sliding scales. Always confirm the current figure directly, since the headline number may not include registration or materials fees.

Can I use an ESA or scholarship in Missouri?

Yes. Missouri operates MOScholars, an Empowerment Scholarship Accounts program funded by tax-credit donations and run through the State Treasurer's office. Qualifying families can use the award toward tuition at participating private schools and microschools, so check eligibility and award amounts before you enroll.

What types of microschools are in Missouri?

The eight listings break down into three Montessori programs, three learner-driven schools, and two general microschools. Guidepost Montessori operates two of the Montessori campuses, and learner-driven options include Origins KC in Liberty and Wonder KC in Kansas City. Most enroll small mixed-age groups rather than single-grade classrooms.

Where should I start looking in Missouri?

St. Louis is the clearest starting point, with three listings including Saint Louis Sudbury School. The Kansas City area is the other main hub, so families on the western side of the state can look at Origins KC and Wonder KC without a long drive.
