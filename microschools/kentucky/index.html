Microschooling & school choice in Kentucky

This directory currently lists two Kentucky programs across two cities, Louisville and Lexington. The mix is even: one Montessori school and one microschool, reflecting how small the formal microschool sector still is statewide. Tuition is not posted for these listings, so families need to request current rates directly from each program. Kentucky has no active statewide ESA or voucher program for private tuition. A 2022 tax-credit scholarship law was struck down by the state Supreme Court, and voters turned down a 2024 constitutional amendment that would have opened public funding to non-public schools. For now, parents generally cover costs themselves or rely on private scholarships.

Finding the right fit in Kentucky

Start with the two cities that have listings: Louisville and Lexington. Magnolia Blossom Montessori sits in Louisville and Sphinx Academy in Lexington, so a parent can reach either within most of the state's population centers. Because Kentucky has no statewide ESA or voucher for private tuition right now, budget on the assumption that you pay directly, then ask each program about sliding-scale rates or private scholarships. On a visit, ask about group size, the daily schedule, how teachers track progress, and what a typical week looks like. Confirm whether the program registers as a private school or operates as a homeschool co-op, since that affects attendance reporting and your filing obligations.

Kentucky microschool FAQ

How many microschools are in Kentucky?

This directory lists two microschools and homeschool co-ops in Kentucky, one in Louisville and one in Lexington. That count reflects programs we currently track, not every option in the state, so it is worth checking back as new schools register.

How much does a microschool cost in Kentucky?

Cost is set by each individual program. Tuition is not posted for the two Kentucky listings, so you will need to contact Magnolia Blossom Montessori or Sphinx Academy directly for current rates. Many small programs offer payment plans or limited scholarships, so ask.

Can I use an ESA or scholarship in Kentucky?

Not for private tuition at the moment. Kentucky has no active statewide ESA or voucher program: a 2022 tax-credit scholarship law was overturned by the state Supreme Court, and voters rejected a 2024 amendment that would have allowed public money for non-public schools. Check the Kentucky Department of Education for any current updates.

What types of microschools are in Kentucky?

The two listings split between a Montessori school and a microschool. Montessori programs use mixed-age classrooms and self-directed work, while microschools tend to run small multi-grade groups with a flexible curriculum. Visiting both helps you compare teaching styles.

Which Kentucky cities have microschools listed?

Both listings sit in Kentucky's largest metro areas. Magnolia Blossom Montessori is in Louisville and Sphinx Academy is in Lexington, which together cover much of the state's population. Families outside those two cities may need to travel or consider a homeschool co-op model.
