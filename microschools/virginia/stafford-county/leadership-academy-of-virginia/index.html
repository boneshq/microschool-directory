About this school

The Leadership Academy of Virginia is a microschool located in Stafford County serving students in grades one through six. The institution adopts a student-centered educational model with a holistic perspective, emphasizing personalized and purposeful learning experiences. The curriculum is grounded in core values designed to foster confidence and individual growth among young learners within the community.

Meet the founder: Hello Family

Michele Stewart is the founder of Leadership Academy of Virginia. A master’s in education holder with over a decade of classroom experience, she established the school to address systemic educational shortcomings and support diverse student needs.
