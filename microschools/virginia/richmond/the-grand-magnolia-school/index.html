About this school

The Grand Magnolia School operates as a microschool in Richmond, Virginia, serving students across various grade levels. The institution utilizes small cohorts of ten to twelve children, maintaining a teacher-to-student ratio of four to one or better. Its distinct approach emphasizes individualized instruction and experiential learning, allowing students to progress at their own pace while exploring unique interests within a community-focused environment.
