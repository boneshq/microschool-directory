Project-Based

Iron Village Preparatory Academy serves K-8 students in Richmond, Virginia, through a microschool model focused on academic empowerment, cultural celebration, and community engagement. Its distinct approach emphasizes individualized instruction and experiential learning, allowing students to progress at their own pace while exploring unique interests within a community-focused environment.
