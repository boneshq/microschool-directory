About this school

Blossoming Imaginations is a microschool in Leesburg, Virginia, serving Kindergarten through 2nd grade. The program utilizes a nature-inspired, project-based curriculum that integrates hands-on exploration with academic instruction. Distinctive features include regular outdoor activities, art, and storytelling designed to support whole-child development. The school employs a small-group setting to provide individualized instruction tailored to each student’s specific strengths and learning style.

Meet the founder: Chavon Parker

Chavon Parker owns Blossoming Imaginations. A former teacher with dyslexia and a background in graphic communications and animation, she previously taught Pre-K and kindergarten in private school settings, leveraging her experience to support diverse learning needs.
