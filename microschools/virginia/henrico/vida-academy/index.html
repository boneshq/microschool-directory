About this school

Vida Academy is a bilingual microschool located in Henrico, Virginia, serving students from PreK through 3rd grade (ages 4–9). The program immerses children in both English and Spanish to build academic foundations while fostering early bilingualism. Classes are small and supportive, integrating social-emotional learning with cultural awareness. Additionally, the curriculum emphasizes nature-based education, providing regular opportunities for outdoor exploration and hands-on science experiences for all enrolled students.

Meet the founder: Hi

Amanda Simmons-Polanco, founder of Vida Academy, brings nearly 15 years of educational experience. She previously taught in New York City and served as founding principal at a Manhattan elementary school, leveraging her background as a bilingual English-Spanish speaker to support personalized learning environments.
