About this school

Solstice Hybrid Academy serves grades K through 4 in Norfolk, Virginia, offering a distinctive mobile learning model for students aged five to ten. The school utilizes a converted school bus as its primary classroom facility, enabling daily field trips and real-world exploration experiences. This approach facilitates personalized instruction within small group settings. The academy operates on a flexible, year-round schedule that lacks strict attendance requirements, allowing families to maintain adaptable routines while their children engage in an unconventional educational environment.

Meet the founder: Sarah Harrington

Sarah Harrington founded Solstice Hybrid Academy. A former public school substitute and art educator with ten years of experience, she combines academic instruction with mobile enrichment to support her students' learning needs.
