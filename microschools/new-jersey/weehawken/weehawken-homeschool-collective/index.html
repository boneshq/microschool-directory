About this school

The Weehawken Homeschool Collective in Weehawken, New Jersey, serves students across various grade levels through a homeschooling model. The program emphasizes social-emotional and cognitive development within a nurturing environment designed to foster belonging and connection. By prioritizing the exploration of natural curiosity, the collective aims to support each child’s full potential while facilitating self-discovery and engagement with the surrounding world.
