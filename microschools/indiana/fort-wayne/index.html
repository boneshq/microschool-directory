Microschooling & school choice in Fort Wayne

Three microschools serve Fort Wayne in this directory, and they split evenly across three approaches: Montessori, learner-driven, and project-based. Listed tuition starts around $3,200 a year, though costs vary by school and grade level. Indiana runs two main school-choice programs that families here can look into. The Education Scholarship Account (ESA) gives eligible students state funds for private tuition, tutoring, and other approved costs, while the Choice Scholarship voucher helps income-qualified families pay for accredited private schools. Eligibility rules differ between the two, so confirm which one fits your situation before you enroll.

Finding the right fit in Fort Wayne

Start by comparing the three Fort Wayne listings side by side: Kainos Microschool runs a project-based model, Alyssum Montessori follows Montessori methods, and The Wilds: An Acton Academy is learner-driven. Each one teaches differently, so think about which approach fits your child before you tour. Indiana's Education Scholarship Account can reimburse approved expenses like tuition and tutoring, but you apply through the state and the money follows eligibility rules, not the school's say-so. On a visit, ask how many students share a classroom, how teachers track progress without a traditional report card, and whether the school accepts ESA or Choice Scholarship funds. Bring a list of your must-haves and check the state education agency site for current funding deadlines.
