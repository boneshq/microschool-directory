About this school

Riverside Academy Educational Services operates a microschool in Brookville, Franklin County, Indiana, serving students in grades K-6. The program emphasizes small class sizes and personalized instruction within a self-directed learning environment. Scholars engage with customized learning plans tailored to their individual pace and current academic level. This approach prioritizes student choice and voice, aiming to develop critical thinking, creativity, and essential skills through an individualized educational framework.

Meet the founder: Ashley Brodbeck

Ashley Brodbeck founded Riverside Educational Services in Indiana in 2023. She holds bachelor’s and master’s degrees in education from Northern Kentucky University and Brandman University, with ten years of teaching experience across Ohio, Indiana, and California.
