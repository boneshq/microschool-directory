About this school

Batesville Enrichment Center serves students from kindergarten through twelfth grade in Batesville, Indiana. The institution provides homeschool enrichment classes, elective courses, camps, clubs, and tutoring services for the local community and surrounding areas. This microschool offers a variety of academic and extracurricular opportunities designed to support diverse educational needs across all grade levels within the region.
