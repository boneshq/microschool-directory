About this school

Bridges Microschool in Granger, Indiana, serves students in grades K through 6th grade. This hybrid institution utilizes a multi-age setting to provide individualized instruction and strong academics within small class sizes. The curriculum emphasizes literacy, math, project-based learning, creativity, movement, and real-world experiences. By focusing on hands-on activities and personalized support, the school aims to help students develop confidence, independence, and lifelong success habits in a relationship-centered environment.

Meet the founder: Amy Zache

Amy Zache founded Bridges Learning Lab and Bridges Microschool in Granger, Indiana. With nearly 20 years of experience as a classroom teacher, reading interventionist, and literacy specialist for grades K–6, she established these institutions to provide personalized academic support.
