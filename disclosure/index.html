microschool.directory is free for families, and basic school listings are free. This page explains how the directory earns money and how that is kept separate from what we show you.

Paid upgrades for schools

We may offer schools optional paid upgrades. A Pro upgrade adds profile features and reporting for the school. A Featured upgrade places a school in a spot labeled "Featured" on its city and state pages, with a limited number per city.

Payment never changes a school's "Verified" status, never changes the order of non-featured results, and never buys a place in our editorial guides.

Affiliate links

Some links to curricula, classes and services may be affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links are marked where they appear. We include a resource because we think it is useful to families, not because of its commission rate.

Editorial guides

Our state guides and "best microschools" lists are editorial selections. Schools cannot pay to be included or to change what we write.

Sponsored content

If we publish content a company paid for, it will be labeled "Sponsored".

We follow the FTC's guidance on endorsements and advertising. Questions: hello@microschool.directory.
