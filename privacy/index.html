microschool.directory ("we", "us") is a free directory that helps families find microschools, homeschool co-ops and learning resources in the United States. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

  • Requests to a school. When you use "Request info" on a listing, we collect your name, email address, an optional phone number, the grade your learner is entering (optional) and your message. We do not ask for your child's name.
  • School listings and claims. When you add or claim a school, we collect the school details you enter, your contact email and phone, any photos you upload, and later edits. School details are published on the listing. Your contact email is used to review the submission, set up owner access and send you account emails; it is not published.
  • Owner accounts. Your email address, used to sign in and manage listings you own.
  • Community posts. What you post in the community forum, and the name you choose to show with it, is public.
  • Messages. Anything you send to hello@microschool.directory.

Information collected automatically

  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used: pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser, the site that referred you, and actions such as opening a school's website, starting a call or email, or completing a form. Google Analytics uses cookies or similar identifiers. We do not send your name, email address, phone number or message to Google Analytics.
  • Spam protection. Our forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to check that a person, not a bot, is submitting. Cloudflare processes browser and device signals to do this.
  • Service logs. Our hosting and database providers process IP addresses and request details to deliver and protect the site. Listing and claim submissions store the submitting IP address so we can investigate abuse.
  • Content providers. Maps (CARTO, with OpenStreetMap data), fonts (Google Fonts) and code libraries (unpkg, jsDelivr) load from those providers, which receive your IP address when your browser requests them.

How we use information

  • Run the directory, publish and correct listings, and review claims.
  • Pass your request to the school you chose, and help the school reply to you.
  • Answer your messages and send emails about your submission or account.
  • Prevent spam, fraud and abuse, and keep the site secure.
  • Measure and improve the site.
  • Meet legal obligations.

How we share information

  • With the school you contact. When you send a request and check the consent box, we share your name, email address, phone number (if you gave one), learner grade and message with that school so it can reply to you. Once a school receives your request, its own practices govern how it uses your information.
  • With service providers that help us run the site: hosting (GitHub Pages), database, sign-in and file storage (Supabase), spam protection (Cloudflare), analytics (Google), email sending and forwarding (Resend, ForwardEmail), and internal notification tools our team uses to see new submissions. They may use the information only to provide their service to us.
  • For legal and safety reasons, when required by law or to protect people, the site or our rights.
  • In a business transfer, if the directory is sold or reorganized, under this policy's protections.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Affiliate links and paid placements

Some links may be affiliate links, and schools may pay for upgraded or featured listings. See our advertising disclosure. When you follow an affiliate link, the partner may use cookies to credit the referral to us.

Children

microschool.directory is for parents, guardians, educators and school operators. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Requests to schools ask only for a learner's grade. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

How long we keep information

  • Requests to schools: up to 24 months after you send them, then deleted or de-identified.
  • Listing submissions, claims and review decisions: as long as needed to run the directory and keep an accurate record of how listings were reviewed.
  • Analytics data: according to our Google Analytics retention settings.

Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing hello@microschool.directory. We may need to confirm your identity first.
  • School owners can update their listing from their account.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser, or use Google's browser add-on to opt out of Google Analytics.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards for the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes

We will post any update here with a new effective date. If a change materially affects how we use information you already gave us, we will tell you before it applies.

Contact

Questions or requests: hello@microschool.directory.
